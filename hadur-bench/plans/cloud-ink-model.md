# The `cloud-ink-model` card (BENCH-293)

The bench shall carry the cloud model of Sepia's ink in `data/tools/ink_model/`, a read that plays no battle and ships nothing. It applies the engine's collision order as `hadur117.core.move.BulletShadows` states it (a certain shadow counts 1, a possible one a half, and a meeting only counts before the wave can meet the body) to four questions of [docs/sepia-design.md](../../docs/sepia-design.md): how much of a still body one 0.1 bullet shades by the sidestep between where it is fired and where the body lands (`e1.py`); what up to three such bullets shade along a dash (`e2.py`); what a stream of them shades for a body that commits one stretch of path per enemy shot, against an even spread of enemy bullets (`e5.py`); and how often the census's own offline guns find that body and get through the ink, with every choice's forecast held to the path as it was then run (`e6.py`). The runs the doc quotes are saved beside the scripts, each script reproduces its file exactly, and `data/tools/test_ink_model.py` holds the geometry and the quoted numbers to them.

Read so far: one shot shades 7%, 35%, 63% and 91% of a still body at a 0, 10, 20 and 30 degree sidestep; a body that inks every time its gun is cool is 48% shaded against an even spread (53% when it turns cleanly at the ends of its track), against 1.5% for attack fire and 2.6% for attack fire bent for shadow; a census-grade guess-factor gun finds the body with 15% of its bullets and gets 5% through the ink, a segmented one 22% and 7%; and a gun whose aim the body can reproduce lands 0.2% of its bullets if it is head-on and 1.8% if it is linear, against the pick that takes the option its bullet misses.

Still to run on this card: the geometry against 200 bullet meetings recorded from the engine's truth logs. It passes when every recorded meeting falls inside a modelled shadow, and it stops on the first one that does not.
