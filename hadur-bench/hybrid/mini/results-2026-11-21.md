## What it says

- **Gun: JayLexi's is the best of the three.** On the same movement, swapping JayLexi's gun out
  loses every time: FoilistMC's gun −0.62 and CunobelinDC's −0.33 on JayLexi's movement; on
  FoilistMC's movement JayLexi's gun is +0.43 over FoilistMC's own and CunobelinDC's −0.34.
  FoilistMC's and CunobelinDC's guns are level with each other (±0.35 either way).
- **Movement: JayLexi's is the weakest.** With JayLexi's gun, FoilistMC's movement is +0.47; with
  FoilistMC's gun, +0.66; with CunobelinDC's gun, CunobelinDC's movement is +0.58 over JayLexi's.
  FoilistMC's and CunobelinDC's movements are close.
- **Best + best = FoilistMC movement + JayLexi gun, about 89.0** (89.40 paired off FoilistMC's live
  88.97), level with or just above the MiniRumble's 1st. The whole route to 1st for JayLexi is a
  movement as good as FoilistMC's or CunobelinDC's; the gun is not the gap.
- CunobelinDC's mid-table damage edge is not its gun's aim: its gun is no better than FoilistMC's
  and worse than JayLexi's here, so any edge there is power or movement.
