## Notice

1. **The cost showed up in the rumble, not in the tests.** A design can pass everything and still
   slide. The lesson of this lab is to add the count (`HL-40`) before the slide is felt.
2. **A guess you can later correct.** Hadur's allowance starts as a guess and is corrected by the
   first fact the engine gives. Yours is a constant; exercise 2 is a step towards Hadur's.
3. **Every claim has a bench.** The R8 report is a table with conditions and numbers, the same
   shape as the reports from your lab 12 bench.

That is the last lab of the course. The capstone asks you to take one feature through the whole
cycle on your own: a requirement, a failing test, the code, the guardrails and a bench to prove it.
