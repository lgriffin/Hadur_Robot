# Exercises

1. **A missing cause.** Our own bullets can also collide with the enemy's bullets in mid-air. What
   does that do to the enemy's energy? To ours? Does the ledger need to know?
2. **A hit on us with no refund.** Change `enemyBulletHitUs` to ignore the refund and run the
   property. Which interval does jqwik shrink to? Is that the one you expected?
3. **The edge.** Set `TOLERANCE` to 0 and run the tests. Which fail, and why? What does that tell
   you about comparing floating-point numbers?
4. **Two shots in one interval.** The enemy can fire at most once every few ticks, but what happens
   to the ledger if we miss a scan and two shots land in one interval? Write the test. What would
   the ledger report, and is that acceptable?
5. **Property or example?** The `limitsAreShots` test is an example. Could you make it a property?
   What would the generator look like, and would it be worth it?
6. **Collision damage.** Robots that ram each other also lose energy. Add the cause to the table in
   step 1, write the `HL-n` requirement for it, and decide who tells the ledger.
