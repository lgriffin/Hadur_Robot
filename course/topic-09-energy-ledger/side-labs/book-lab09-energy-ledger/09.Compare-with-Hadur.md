  the scenarios, tagged with requirement IDs the same way as yours.

## Notice

1. **The truth first.** Both properties build the world, then compute the symptom, then check the
   ledger recovers the world. That is the pattern to reach for whenever a function is an inverse.
2. **An invariant says more than a measurement.** A property that states what must always be true
   finds a wrong ledger without waiting for a battle to look odd.
3. **One owner.** Only the core reads the ledger, and an ArchUnit rule says so.
