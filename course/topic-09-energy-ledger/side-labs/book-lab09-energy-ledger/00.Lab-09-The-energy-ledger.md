# Lab 09: The energy ledger

In lab 08 the robot treated every energy drop between 0.1 and 3.0 as an enemy shot. That is wrong
as soon as we hit the enemy: our own bullet takes energy off it, and the robot dodges a bullet
that was never fired. In this lab you write an **energy ledger** that accounts for every change in
the enemy's energy, and test it with a property that mixes every cause in every order.

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you can:

- list every reason an enemy's energy can change between two scans
- write a ledger that subtracts the causes it knows and reads what is left as a shot
- write a jqwik generator that builds a battle where you know the truth, and check the ledger
  recovers it
- show that a flaw in an earlier lab was a real bug, with a failing scenario

## What you start from

`course-labs/lab-08`, or this lab's `archives/start.zip`.

## What you will have at the end

- `hadurling.core.ledger.EnergyLedger`, which `Core` now uses in place of the naive detector
- requirements `HL-13` (reworded) and `HL-14` to `HL-16`
- unit tests, a mixed-events property and two Cucumber scenarios
- two new ArchUnit rules for the `ledger` package
- a regenerated replay fixture

This is `course-labs/lab-09`.
