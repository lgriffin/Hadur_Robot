# The hole in lab 08

A bullet of power `p` costs the shooter `p` energy. So when the enemy's energy drops by 1.5 between
two scans, it probably fired a power 1.5 bullet. "Probably" is the problem. List every reason the
enemy's energy can change between two scans:

| Cause | Effect on the enemy's energy |
|-------|------------------------------|
| It fires a bullet of power `p` | falls by `p` |
| Our bullet of power `p` hits it | falls by `4p`, plus `2 * (p - 1)` more if `p > 1` |
| Its bullet hits us | rises by `3p` (a refund) |
| It hits a wall at speed `v` | falls by `max(0, |v| / 2 - 1)` |

Lab 08 only knew the first line. Any of the others looks like a shot, or hides one. A hit by our
bullet of power 0.5 takes 2.0 off the enemy, and lab 08 would build a wave of power 2.0 out of it.
The surfer then dodges a bullet that does not exist, and wastes the move.

## Make the bug visible first

Before you fix anything, write the scenario that shows the bug. Add it to
`features/hadurling.feature`:

```gherkin
@HL-16
Scenario: Our own hit explains a drop
  Given the core has seen an enemy with 100 energy
  And our bullet of power 0.5 hits the enemy
  When the enemy is next seen with 98 energy
  Then the surfer watches 0 enemy waves
```

Add the steps to `CoreSteps` (they are in step 6). Run `mvn -B test` and watch this scenario fail
against lab 08's detector. A bug with a failing test is a bug you can prove you fixed.

The plan: keep a running account of what we know happened, take it out of the raw drop, and read
what is left. If what is left is a legal bullet power (0.1 to 3.0) it is a shot.
