# Compare with Hadur

Hadur's physics lives in the package `hadur2.core.physics`. It is the same idea at the same
shape, grown over time:

- [`Angles`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/physics/Angles.java):
  the same two normalisations, line for line. Read its class comment: "bit-for-bit identical to
  `robocode.util.Utils`", the same promise your property test makes.
- [`Rules`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/physics/Rules.java):
  the bullet formulas and, as well, the speed and turn-rate constants and the energy rules the
  energy ledger needs later.
- [`PhysicsMatchesEngineProperties`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/test/java/hadur2/core/physics/PhysicsMatchesEngineProperties.java):
  your `AnglesMatchEngineProperties`, with more cases: infinities, NaN, plus and minus zero, and
  every `Rules` method.
- [`WallStopTest`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/test/java/hadur2/core/physics/WallStopTest.java):
  tests for the wall prediction in exercise 3, taken to the end.

## Things to notice

1. **The tests are the specification.** Hadur's `Angles` is short because the property test
   pins its behaviour. Nobody dares "improve" it, because the build says no.
2. **Hadur carries a copy of the rules** instead of calling the engine's `robocode.Rules`, for
   the reason you met in step 2: its core may not import Robocode. The property test is what
   keeps the copy honest.
3. **The same tolerance habits.** Find where the tests use `0.0` and where `1e-9`. Ask why for
   each.

The next lab takes the step that makes this separation a rule: the values that cross the
boundary between the engine and the brain.
