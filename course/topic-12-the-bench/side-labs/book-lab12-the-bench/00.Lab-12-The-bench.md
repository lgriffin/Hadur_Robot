# Lab 12: The bench

"It feels better" is not a measurement. In this lab you build a **bench**: a program that fights
Hadurling against other robots in headless Robocode, many times, and reports the score share as a
mean with a 95% interval. To compare two versions it fights both on the **same seeds** and looks
only at the differences. The numbers, the interval and the pairing are plain Java you can unit test.
The battles themselves you run by hand.

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you can:

- explain why a single battle proves nothing, and what a seed is
- compute a mean and a 95% confidence interval with Student's t, and say what happens with one sample
- compare two versions by pairing seeds, and say why that is sharper than comparing two averages
- run a Robocode battle from code with `robocode.control`, in a JVM of its own
- keep slow, random battles out of `mvn verify` and the arithmetic in it
