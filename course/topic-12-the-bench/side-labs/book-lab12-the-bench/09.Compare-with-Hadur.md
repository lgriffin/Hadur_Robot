the ruler that every later change was measured with.

  what the output looks like for a whole opponent set. Read its notes: they say what to move.

## Notice

1. **Share, not wins.** Hadur's report says score share is the number to move, because a robot can
   win a round and still lose the match on damage.
2. **Every report states its conditions.** Rounds, seeds, Java version, whether the data was wiped.
   A number without its conditions cannot be compared with another.
3. **The ruler comes first.** Hadur built the bench before the changes it would judge. You have
   done the same: from the next lab on, a claim about a change can be checked.
