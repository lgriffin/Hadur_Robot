## Questions to answer

Write short answers in your bench report or a `docs/capstone.md`.

1. **Trigger.** How is Hadur's trigger stricter than yours? What evidence did it use to choose the numbers?
2. **Default.** What does Hadur do when its trigger is off? Is your default the same?
3. **Tests.** Which of Hadur's tests have no counterpart in yours? Pick the one you wish you had written.
4. **Guardrails.** Which guardrail did Hadur's stage touch that yours did not (replay fixtures, a re-pinned duel snapshot, the round record)?
5. **Bench.** What did Hadur's gate include that yours did not? Did it include a regression set of strong robots?
6. **Verdict.** If you got "no measurable change" and Hadur got a gain, what is different about the opponents, the conditions or the feature?
7. **Release note.** Read `v3.5.md` again. What does it say that yours does not?

## Where to go next

- Read `data/learnings.md`. Each entry is a finding a later stage would otherwise have to rediscover. Write one for your feature, in the same form: an ID, a status (confirmed, open or refuted) and the evidence.
- Take the feature a step further and bench it again. Hadur's 3.5.1 moved MIR-1's aim to the planned position and made rammers reverse, after the first release.
- Pick another option from step 1 and repeat the loop. The second run is much faster.

You have now built, tested, benched and shipped a feature the way Hadur's stages were. That is the course.
