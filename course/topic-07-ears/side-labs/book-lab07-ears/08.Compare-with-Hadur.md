## Notice

1. **Stages.** Hadur's test treats a requirement as due only when its stage is reached (S1 to S7
   for the duel, M0 to M6 for melee, R0 to R9 for the climb). That is how a plan could list the
   next stage's requirements in advance, with the build red only when the stage arrives. It is
   exercise 4.
2. **The planner and the implementer.** In Hadur, a planning session writes the requirement IDs
   and acceptance criteria, and an implementing session builds them. The ID is what they share.
   The traceability test is the referee.
3. **Requirements are tested at every layer.** `CORE-1` is an ArchUnit rule; `CORE-2` is a replay
   and a property; `RES-1` is the guard test. The tag does not care which layer.

That finishes Part 3. The next part is the strategy: waves, guess factors and the guns that
learn. Everything there arrives the way you have just practised: a requirement, a failing test, the code,
and a proof.
