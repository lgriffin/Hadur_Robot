# Lab 11: Holding back on thin evidence

A robot that changes its behaviour on a rate needs to know how much to trust the rate. One hit in
one shot is a hit rate of 100%, and nobody should fire full power on the strength of it. In this lab
every rate Hadurling reads comes with a **margin of error**, and two policies use it: one chooses the
bullet power, one decides how long to trust a remembered profile. Both are built to do nothing until
the evidence is good enough, and the tests prove it.

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you can:

- compute a 95% interval for a rate with the Agresti-Coull method, and say which interval you use
- keep a rolling window of recent outcomes and test it against the obvious slow way
- write a policy that holds back when the evidence is thin, and test both sides of its line
- find a rule that can never fire, and write the test that would have caught it
- explain why `Estimate`, `HitWindow` and the policies are pure and depend on nothing
