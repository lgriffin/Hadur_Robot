## Notice

1. **A margin is part of the number.** Hadur never passes a bare rate to a decision.
2. **A rule that never fires looks like a rule that works.** Every test of the hold-back passes. Only
   a test of reachability finds the dead rule.
3. **Evidence outlives the round.** A round is too short to judge a rate, so a window has to span
   rounds. Look for where Hadur keeps its windows between rounds, and compare with your `static`
   `Evidence`.
