# Compare with Hadur

Hadur has the same three values, with the same names but a `Bot` prefix, in the package
`hadur2.core.model`:

- [`BotInput`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/model/BotInput.java):
  your `Input`, with more fields (gun cooling rate, how many opponents there are). Look at how
  it copies the event list.
- [`BotEvent`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/model/BotEvent.java):
  your `Event`. A closed hierarchy of about a dozen types, written exactly the way you wrote
  yours. Find `Scan`, `HitWall` and `RobotDeath`, and the ones Hadur adds: `BulletHitBullet`,
  `SkippedTurn`, `TickTime`.
- [`BotOrders`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/model/BotOrders.java):
  your `Orders`, with NaN meaning "leave unchanged", a `NONE`, and a `Builder`. Its class comment
  explains why `equals` uses `Double.compare`, in the words of step 4.

The tick as a whole is described in
[`docs/architecture.md`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/docs/architecture.md),
in the section "The tick". It has the same four parts as step 6.

## Notice

1. **Same shape, bigger.** Hadur's values grew from your list to fit a stronger strategy; the
   style never changed.
2. **The robot class is still only a translator.** Open `hadur2/Hadur.java` again and find
   `input()` and `apply()`. They are your versions, with a few more fields.
3. **`BotEvent` and the engine's order of delivery.** The engine delivers events in its own
   priority order; Hadur's brain handles them in the order they arrive. Your list is ordered, so
   you could do the same.

Lab 04 gives the brain its own module that physically cannot import Robocode, so that this
boundary is enforced by the build, not by habit.
