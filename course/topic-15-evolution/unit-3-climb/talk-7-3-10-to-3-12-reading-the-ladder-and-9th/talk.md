# 3.10 to 3.12: reading the ladder, and 9th

3.10 lengthened the shield list and fell to 20th on a rough pass, which taught Hadur to read a live pass for drift before trusting its rank. A whole-field census retired duress for 3.11, and a latch bug that silenced the gun against mirror bots, fixed in 3.12, gave Hadur its first top-10 place.
