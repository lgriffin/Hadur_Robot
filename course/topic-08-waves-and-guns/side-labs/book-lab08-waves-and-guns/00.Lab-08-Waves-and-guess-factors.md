# Lab 08: Waves, guess factors and nearest neighbours

Until now Hadurling aims head-on and moves in a plain orbit. In this lab it learns. Every bullet
becomes a **wave**, an expanding circle. When a wave reaches the enemy we record one number, the
**guess factor**: where the enemy went, as a share of where it could have gone. A small k-d tree
finds the past situations that look most like this one, and the gun fires where the enemy most
often went. The same wave class lets the movement dodge the enemy's shots.

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you can:

- explain a wave and a guess factor, and convert one into the other and back
- write a k-d tree and prove it correct against a brute-force search with a jqwik property
- add a learning gun that falls back to head-on until it has enough samples
- add a surfer that picks the safer direction when an enemy wave is coming
- say why a replay fixture is regenerated on purpose, and do it
