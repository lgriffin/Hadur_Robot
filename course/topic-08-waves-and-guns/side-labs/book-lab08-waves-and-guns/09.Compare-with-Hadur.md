# Compare with Hadur

Hadur has the same pieces at full size, refined over many releases. The links below are pinned to
release 3.5.1.

- [`KdTree`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/knn/KdTree.java):
  the same idea, with buckets that split when they fill. Find the bucket size and compare with
  your sorted-insert exercise.
- [`Wave`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/model/Wave.java):
  compare its guess factor with yours. What does it do that yours does not?
- [`MainGun`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/gun/MainGun.java):
  the learning gun. Look for the minimum-samples rule and the way the features are scaled.
- [`SurfMover`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/move/SurfMover.java):
  the surfer. It looks at many more futures than clockwise and anticlockwise.
- [`BulletShadows`](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/main/java/hadur2/core/move/BulletShadows.java)
  and its [property test](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/a9ee021/hadur-core/src/test/java/hadur2/core/move/BulletShadowsProperties.java):
  where a model-based property earns its keep on a hard piece of geometry.

## Notice

1. **The same wave serves both sides.** Hadur's `Wave` also serves the gun and the mover.
2. **Learning from every wave.** The gun does not wait for a hit. A virtual hit is as good a lesson.
3. **Simple first.** Your `HL-13` is a naive detector. Hadur began that way too, and the next lab
   shows what replaced it.
