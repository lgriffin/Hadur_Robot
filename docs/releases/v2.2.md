Hadur 2.2: the finished Hadur 2 duelist, with melee.

**Download:** `hadur2.Hadur_2.2.jar` below. Robocode name `hadur2.Hadur 2.2`; runs on Java 11 or later. Enter it in both the RoboRumble (1v1) and the MeleeRumble: see [docs/rumble-submission.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/rumble-submission.md).

New since 2.1, one on one:

- **Opponent memory (S3):** a crash-safe profile per opponent, kept within Robocode's data quota.
- **Recognise and adapt (S4):** gun and movement tiers from the profile pick the opening gun, the surf prior and the flattener, and stored samples are replayed at reduced weight until live evidence disagrees.
- **Aggression (S5):** a distance controller that comes in only while Hadur clearly out-hits the enemy, full-power shots against guns that cannot hit it, and finishing and ramming a beaten enemy.
- **Bullet-shielding counter:** an enemy that shoots our bullets down is detected and our aim is jittered past its shield (Saguaro: 1.7% to 68.8% score share).
- **Unhittable (S6):** our bullets in flight shadow the enemy's waves, and the surf treats the shadowed angles as safe; the movement changes flavour when a known opponent hits more than usual; a tick budget sheds work after slow ticks and skipped turns.
