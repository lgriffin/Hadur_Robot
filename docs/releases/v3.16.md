Hadur 3.16 is a test release of the first 1v1 per-opponent mode table and the team's stage 1, both
benched against 3.15. Melee plays as 3.15.

- **1v1: mode table v1 (SHIELD-16 on, 49 robots join the shield list).** The shield gets Saguaro's
  body-turn aim base (head-on from where the enemy stood on the tick before it fired, plus its body
  turn on the firing tick), and the 49 robots it beat normal mode against on a screen and on 8 fresh
  seeds join the shield list (14 + 83 + 103 + 49 = 249). Census against 3.15 (census-312, 2 seeds,
  7,272 battles, A/A −0.02 ± 0.17): **+0.54 ± 0.20 APS** (lower end +0.34). By segment: ranks
  51-400 +0.27, ranks 401+ +0.16, large losses +0.06, rammers +0.04, top 24 0.00. Results in PR
  #244, ship in PR #245. Duel only: shield mode runs only in a duel; melee and team replays are
  unchanged.
- **Team: stage 1, the gun's depth 21 and shooting rammers (MGUN-9, MGUN-10, team only).** Team
  census over the 46 teams against 3.15: +3.4 ± 0.9 APS (PR #226, shipped in PR #227).
- **Team: no per-team mode table.** The team mode table (SOFT, GANG, FINISH, LEARN, PACK, SCREEN,
  POOL) failed its census (PR #246): the 5 locked teams gave +0.2 ± 0.8 points over the 46 teams
  against a +3.0 bar, so every team mode stays off.
- **Bench expectation and noise.** The bench overpredicted 3.15 by about 0.5 APS in 1v1, and one
  live pass is good to about ±0.4 APS, so read 3.16 by segment after its first full pass, not by
  the headline.
