Hadur 3.6 is the architecture evolution's first release (stages A0 to A2). It changes how the robot is built, not how it plays.

- **One kernel, separate strands.** The duel moves out of the 2,000-line core into its own controller behind a role contract, beside the melee brain. Every package has one owner and a hash pin, and the conductor hands each role only what it may see.
- **A charter fixed at tick 0.** The battle's facts choose Duel, Melee or Team before the first tick, and within a round the role only steps down.
- **The same play.** All eleven recorded battles (1v1, melee, sentry, hand-off, warm and duress) replay to identical orders, telemetry and data files. On the bench A2 is level with 3.5.1 within noise; see [docs/bench/a2-ab.md](../bench/a2-ab.md).

The planned rumble upload is 3.7, at the end of A5, which adds team play and a team jar. 3.6 is published so the A2 build has a release of its own:
