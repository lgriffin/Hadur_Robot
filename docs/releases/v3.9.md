Hadur 3.9 gives back what 3.5's rammer escape cost against close-range fighters. The live-loser
bisect put the only resolved regression since 3.4 in 3.5: robots such as TopGun, Silver, Ice and
Predator confirm as rammers, and running from them Hadur was hit two to seven times as often
([live-loser bisect](../bench/local/2026-10-07_live-loser-bisect.md)).

- **The rammer trial (RAM-3).** Once a robot confirms as a rammer, each round plays one way from
  its start: the escape (RAM-2) or standing and fighting (RAM-1 only, as 3.4 played). The escape
  is tried for two rounds, then Hadur plays whichever way has ended its rounds with the better
  energy margin. Pure rammers keep being escaped; fighters that 3.4 beat are fought again.
- **Gates against 3.8.5, engine 1.11.1** ([candidate-39-gate](../bench/local/2026-10-07_candidate-39-gate.md)):
  live losers **+5.46** (clustered +3.08 to +7.84, ten opponents up after Holm, WaveRammer +22,
  Predator +21, Silver +20.5, TopGun +20); leak set level (equivalent within one point); top 20
  level (+0.00 ± 1.20); 3.5's rammers level as a set, with RamFire and Relentless 0.6 to 0.8 lower.
