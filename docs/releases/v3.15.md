Hadur 3.15 ships the two 1v1 changes that survived the top-5 census. It is 3.14 with two changes,
made only in a duel. 3.14 was never released, so against the live 3.13 it also brings 3.14's
melee gun (MGUN-7) and the team's shared gun learning (MGUN-8); both are listed below. The factorial is in PR #221 and the release-candidate census in PR #222
(plan: [docs/plan-top5.md](../plan-top5.md)).

- **The shield builds a wave from an uncertain shot (SHIELD-14, `wall`).** When the ledger cannot
  tell an enemy shot from a wall hit on the same scan, the shield now guesses its power and
  shields it, instead of letting it through unshielded. Factorial on 1,212 bots: +0.19 ± 0.09
  APS, all of it on ranks 401+.
- **The surf keeps the two flattener views off (MOVE-24).** Against guns that learn, the
  flatteners were meant to make Hadur's visits flat; on the field they cost more than they won.
  Factorial: +0.12 ± 0.10 APS.
- **Census of both together against 3.14** (1,212 bots, 2 seeds, paired, with an A/A arm at
  −0.00 ± 0.17): **+0.34 ± 0.15 APS**. By segment: rammers −0.00, the 719 weak-tail bots +0.16,
  the 88 large losses +0.07, ranks 51-400 +0.12, the top 24 −0.02. RAM-4 (the rammer heat wave)
  was censused beside it at +0.28, below the pair alone, and is not shipped.
- **The 1v1 changes leave melee and team alone.** Both switches act only when the battle started
  as a duel; every melee and team replay fixture passes as in 3.14. The one 1v1 fixture whose
  orders moved, `abc.Shadow_3.83c`, was re-recorded on 3.15.
- **Melee, new against live 3.13: the melee gun draws on 12 past situations per opponent, not 3
  (MGUN-7, from 3.14).** Melee census: +0.88 ± 0.19 APS on the cloud (413 opponents) and +1.15 ±
  0.25 on the PC (46 fields, 42 ahead). See [v3.14](v3.14.md).
- **Team, new against live 3.13: each member's gun learns from its teammates' sightings
  (MGUN-8, team only).** Cloud screen over the team top 20: +1.5 ± 1.8 points, not significant
  and never census-checked. The team's gun depth stays at 3 (the k12/k21 depth screens are not
  shipped).
- **Bench expectation:** about +0.3 APS in 1v1 over 3.13 (7th at 89.50), about +1 APS in melee
  over 3.13 (17th at 65.58), team about level with 3.13 (9th at 68.30).
