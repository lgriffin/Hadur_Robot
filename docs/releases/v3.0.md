Hadur 3.0: the Hadur 2 duelist, with a new melee brain.

**Download:** `hadur2.Hadur_3.0.jar`. Robocode name `hadur2.Hadur 3.0`; runs on Java 11 or later. Enter it in both the RoboRumble (1v1) and the MeleeRumble in place of 2.2: see [docs/rumble-submission.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/rumble-submission.md).

One on one, 3.0 is 2.2: the duel's sources are pinned byte for byte (`DuelIdentityTest`), and the duel bench is unchanged within its interval.

New since 2.2, in melee (the Melee Extension Plan, M0 to M6):

- **A gate that fails closed (M1):** melee runs only while two or more opponents are alive, no sentry is on the field and the melee code has not faulted this round; otherwise the duel drives. Sentries are never targeted, and their border is treated as a wall.
- **Sensing (M2):** a spinning radar with four or more alive, the stalest opponent rescanned with two or three, dead robots dropped at once, and opponents' shots read from their energy drops.
- **Minimum-risk movement (M3):** 160 candidate points a tick, weighted by each opponent's energy over distance squared, doubled where Hadur would be an opponent's closest robot, with virtual bullets from every recorded shot.
- **A learning field gun (M4):** a kd-tree of each opponent's movement, played forward for every opponent at once, fired at the peak of the combined density; an energy table with exact kill shots; targeting waves that measure the gun on the whole field.
- **Melee memory and the hand-off (M5):** a melee profile per opponent beside its 1v1 profile, and when one opponent is left, its 1v1 profile and its shots in flight go to the duel.
- **Tuning (M6):** the movement weights were swept and are at a local optimum; the posture no longer holds fire or cuts power, which raises bullet damage by about 15% at the same score.
