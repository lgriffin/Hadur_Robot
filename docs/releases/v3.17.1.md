HadurTeam 3.17.1 is a team-only patch: 3.17 plus pack hunting (HUNT, TMODE-13/TMODE-14). Only
the TeamRumble entry moves to it. Hadur 3.17 stays on the RoboRumble and MeleeRumble pages, so
the 1v1 3.17 bench-to-live test keeps its battles. The solo jar in this release plays exactly as
3.17: HUNT acts only while a teammate lives, and the duel and melee fixtures replay bit for bit.

- **Team: pack hunting (TMODE-13, TMODE-14).** Members split two or three to each enemy,
  leaderless and in name order. Each holds a slot at a fixed 350 px from its prey, ranked by
  bearing. The pull is weak (0.3) and leaves the bullets' risk unscaled, so members keep dodging.
- **Gate.** PC card pc-team-hunt (PR #282): all 46 TeamRumble teams, 6 seeds, paired with 3.17.
  HUNT scored **+4.1 ± 0.9** points (A/A -0.4 ± 0.7).
  - Top teams: ShadowTeam +16.5, Aleph +15.3, CombatTeam +12.7, Ogi +8.1. Nightmare -3.2 ± 7.2
    is within noise.
  - Survival rose from 78.1% to 84.8%.
- **Live expectation.** 3.17 reads 74.80 after 65 battles. Team bench gains have carried over
  about 1:1 (step C: bench +2.9, live +2.7), so 3.17.1 projects to about **78.9**. That is above
  Nightmare (77.02, 5th) and Aleph (76.92).
