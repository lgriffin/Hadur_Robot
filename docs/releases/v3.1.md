## Why this release exists

Hadur 3.0's live RoboRumble rating fell off a cliff at about 08:30 UTC on 2026-09-28 (85.5 to
77.2 APS, survival 92.6% to 71.2%, against equally strong opponents on both sides of the
split), and the bench cannot reproduce it. See
[docs/rumble-climb-r4-r6-plan.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/rumble-climb-r4-r6-plan.md)
for the full analysis. R4 investigates the cause directly; this release (R3.5) ships what was
already merged and unreleased so the next day of live battles is itself a test of whether R1's
client-reliability work touches the effect.

New since 3.0 (all previously merged, unreleased):

- **R1, client reliability (TIME-3/4/5, MEM-6/7, DIAL-3):** the tick-allowance learner and
  round-end profile writes were hardened against a slow or loaded client.
- **R2, full share vs weak and mid-table bots (POW-3/4, END-3, RAM-1, WAVE-3):** power policy
  and the rammer response tightened against bots with more energy or a closing rammer, and
  wall-hit-like energy drops are surfed at half weight instead of trusted outright.
- **R3, a gun that hits surfers (GUN-1/2/3/4, POW-5):** the anti-surfer gun's per-gun ratings
  decay instead of averaging over a lifetime, its feature set grew from 9 to 12, aim against a
  surfing target is asymmetric to the target's own angular width, and a third hybrid gun blends
  the two anti-surfer guns on a live-verdict switch.

## Bench

Cold, 800x600, Robocode 1.9.5.6, security manager on.

- Weak set (`weak-pbi.txt`, 35 rounds x 2 seeds): 838 of 840 rounds won across all 12
  opponents (2 single-round losses, both within the usual variance of this bench — see
  [docs/bench/rumble-3.1-weak-pbi.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/bench/rumble-3.1-weak-pbi.md)).
- Top-10 set (`roborumble-top10.txt`, 35 rounds x 5 seeds): no opponent's score share moved
  outside its own confidence interval from the post-R3 top-50 bench (PR #63); see
  [docs/bench/rumble-3.1-top10.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/bench/rumble-3.1-top10.md).
  No regression beyond noise.

None of R1, R2 or R3's own bench gates were run before this release (`hadur-bench/rumble-sample.txt`
still only covers the top-30 stratum); this release exists to get live data, not to claim
those gates are met.
