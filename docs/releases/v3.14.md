Hadur 3.14 gives the melee gun a wider memory (MGUN-7). It is 3.13 with one change, made only
in free-for-all melee. The [write-up](../bench/melee-gun-3.13.md) has the screens and the census.

- **The melee gun draws on 12 past situations per opponent, not 3.** The density of every
  opponent's likely futures then peaks where several of them overlap, so more shots in the crowd
  land. Census of 3.13's live MeleeRumble field (413 opponents, 46 fields, 2 seeds, paired with
  3.13 on the cloud): **+0.88 ± 0.19 APS**, ahead on 34 of 46 fields, damage per round +8%.
  Ranks 11-414 gain +0.7 to +1.3 each. The top 10 read slightly down (-0.9 ± 0.5 on 9 robots)
  while the top-20 screens were up there (+1.1 and +0.6).
- **1v1 and team play are unchanged.** A 1v1 battle never builds the melee gun, and on a team the
  gun keeps 3 situations. Every 1v1 and team replay fixture passes as in 3.13.
- **Bench expectation:** about +0.9 APS in melee, which is about a third of the 2.7 APS between
  3.13 (17th) and tenth. 1v1 and team ratings should stay where 3.13's are.
