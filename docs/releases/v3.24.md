Hadur 3.24 improves 1v1 and team play. In 1v1 it adds MOVE-52's intercept views and every per-opponent line that
held on the PC since 3.23 (pc-locks rounds 3 to 20). In team play it ships fitted enemy aims on eight teams and the
close-range dodge rerank. Melee code is unchanged: the team changes are team-only. All three entries move to 3.24, following
the rule that minors move together.

- **1v1: 3.23, plus the gains below, each gated on fresh PC seeds.** Apart from MOVE-52, every gain is a per-opponent
  line.

  | Change | PR | Gate read |
  |---|---|---|
  | MOVE-52: enemy bullets our bullets intercept teach the danger views, as a hit does | #926, #934, #935 | +0.04 ± 0.02 and +0.05 ± 0.03 APS on its two panels |
  | pc-locks rounds 3 to 13: shield, still, exit, power, mimic, jitter, ram and dodge lines locked from the field shards | #860 to #952 (banks #861, #868, #871, #896, #903, #906, #914, #923, #930, #943, #952) | each line held on 12 fresh PC seeds |
  | Line audit: two per-opponent lines that cost points come off | #911 | removal |
  | pc-locks rounds 14 to 17: RAM-18 escape bend on 11 rammers, ram-flee, MOVE-56/58 pattern-matcher mimics, SHIELD-33 tiny-shot waves | #964, #968, #975, #978, #980 | each line held on 12 fresh PC seeds |
  | pc-locks rounds 18 and 19: three pattern-matcher mimic lines (pm), one pmx line and two MOVE-67 aim-weight lines | #991, #992 | each line held on 12 fresh PC seeds |
  | pc-locks round 20: MoggFanatic aim weight, BlackIce shield exit off | #994, #995 | +2.4 and +3.5 points (A/A +0.13) |

  - **Whole-field read:** before rounds 18 to 20, master read **+0.54 ± 0.15 APS over the released 3.23** on 1,207 of
    the 1,217 pairings (pc-rc-confirm, #987; A/A inside its band). Rounds 18 to 20 add a few more points on top.
  - **Projection:** the live 3.23 score would become about 93.4. That would put it above Saguaro for 2nd, with
    BeepBoop still about 1.5 ahead, if the bench read carries to the live rumble.
  - **Failed cards** had their code removed, so none of them ships.
- **Team: HadurTeam 3.24 = 3.23 + the changes below.**

  | Change | PR | Gate read |
  |---|---|---|
  | TMODE-50: fitted enemy aims against the eight readable-gun teams | #862, #865, #867 | +0.32 ± 0.10 APS over 46 pairings at 24 fresh seeds |
  | TMODE-52: the close-range dodge reranks 40 candidates through the waves, not 12 | #916, #920 | +0.57 ± 0.41 APS over 45 teams |

  - Together, about +0.9 APS over 3.23, so the live 83.37 (2nd) would become about 84.3. OgiTeam stays 1st (88.02).
  - Endgame power (TMODE-51) and team intercept views (TMODE-53) failed their pilots and were removed.
- **Melee: unchanged.** The team changes run only with a living teammate, and the melee pins moved only for that
  shared code. The MeleeRumble entry (still 3.22 live) moves to 3.24 with the others, so that all three entries
  run one version and the release watch reads melee under 3.24.
- **Isolation.** The team changes do not touch the duel or melee. The duel changes are per-opponent lines plus
  MOVE-52, which runs only in duels.
- **New version, new participant lines.** The package stays `hadur117`. The 3.23 lines come off.
- **No PC clients on the live rumble.**
