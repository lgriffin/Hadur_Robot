Hadur 3.12 fixes a fault that silenced the gun against mirror bots (SHIELD-7). Once Hadur
decided an opponent shoots its bullets down (SHIELD-1, or the verdict stored in the profile by
SHIELD-3), the gun held every aim that carried no anti-shield offset, and MIR-1's mirror aim
never carries one. Against a mirror bot the gun went quiet for the rest of the battle, and the
stored verdict carried that into later battles. 3.12 fires the mirror aim; everything else is
3.11 ([findings](../bench/local/2026-10-11_p312bcd-findings.md), [plan](../plan-3.12.md)).

- **38 volatile bots, against 3.11** (warm replay, the way a live client plays): battles under
  60% score share 14 of 152 to none; **+4.4 to +5.7 points** of share.
- **400-bot screen, against 3.11:** level (+0.03 APS, -0.23 to +0.28).
- **Live expectation:** about +0.05 APS over the field, and fewer large swings against mirror
  bots (MirrorMicro, NaN, Mimic). Read after second battles.
