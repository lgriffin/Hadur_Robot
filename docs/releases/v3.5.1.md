Hadur 3.5.1 fixes three review findings in 3.5's weak-bot responses (R9). Nothing else changes, and none of the three touches play against the top 19, where neither response switches on.

- **MIR-1 aim.** Against a mirror mover, the gun now aims from the position the mirror plan puts Hadur at next tick, not from a straight-line guess. With sentries present it keeps the straight-line guess, because the sentry fence may override the plan.
- **RAM-2 escape.** A rammer charging while driving backward is now modelled moving in its real direction of travel.

On the 11-bot weak set (same 10 seeds), the mean rises from 86.7% to 88.3%. MirrorMicro goes from 83.3% to 91.7% and RedBull from 87.4% to 93.7%. See [docs/bench/r9-weak-leak.md](../bench/r9-weak-leak.md).
