Hadur 3.13 takes Nullstride's shield onto Nullstride's list (SHIELD-13). It also carries the team's
firepower change (MGUN-6). The [PC read-out](../bench/local/2026-10-12_plan90-pc-read.md) and the
[plan](../plan-90.md) have the details.

- **Shield mode plays Nullstride's way.** It never fires an attack shot while shielding (SHIELD-8).
  It learns from the enemy bullets it meets (SHIELD-9), aims with the most frequent offset
  (SHIELD-10), and stays in the mode for the whole round (SHIELD-11). The mode ends for the battle
  at 150 damage taken, or when the enemy is silent. On the 89 robots both lists name: **+4 points a
  robot**, +0.29 APS (8 seeds against 3.12).
- **103 robots join the shield list,** from the 198 on Nullstride's list that were not on
  Hadur's. They were chosen by the D5 rule at 8 seeds and are worth **+1.11 APS** on independent
  seeds. The largest gains are in ranks 11-50: Phoenix, Hydra, Firebird and Chalk are each +34
  to +40 points.
- **Team only (MGUN-6):** with a living teammate and 30 energy or more, a member fires power 3,
  or the power that kills. On the 45 TeamRumble teams that was **+5.7 points a team** (6 seeds).
  1v1 and melee never take this path.
- **Bench expectation:** about +1.4 APS in 1v1. Live kept about 70% of 3.10's shield gain, so
  expect about +1.0. Shield mode acts on nobody off the list, so the rest of the field plays as in
  3.12.
