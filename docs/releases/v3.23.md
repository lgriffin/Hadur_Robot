Hadur 3.23 improves 1v1 and team play. In 1v1 it fixes how bullet shadows feed the danger estimate and adds the
per-opponent lines that held on the PC since 3.22. In team play it ships MOMENTUM's simulated drive on every team.
Melee is unchanged apart from the shadow fix, which reads level there. All three rumbles move to 3.23, following
the rule that minors move together.
