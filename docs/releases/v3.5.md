Hadur 3.5: stop leaking rounds to weak bots. R9 of the climb
([issue #80](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/issues/80),
[bench](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/bench/r9-weak-leak.md)),
on top of 3.4.

**Download:** `hadur2.Hadur_3.5.jar`, attached to this release, at
`https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/releases/download/v3.5/hadur2.Hadur_3.5.jar`.
Robocode name `hadur2.Hadur 3.5`; runs on Java 11 or later. Enter it in both the RoboRumble
(1v1) and the MeleeRumble in place of 3.4: see
[docs/rumble-submission.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/rumble-submission.md).

## Why

3.4 is 20th, but against the 716 robots ranked below 500 it scored 1.7 points under
expectation (about 1.0 APS), mostly by dropping rounds to rammers and mirror movers. The
bench reproduced the losses, so they were tactics, not live-only stalls.

## What changed

- **RAM-2:** once a robot has rammed us (a charge to within 120 px with both robots above
  20 energy) in two different rounds, Hadur runs from it for the rest of the battle: each
  tick it drives the heading that keeps a pursuing robot furthest away over the next 20
  ticks, and fires full power. 3.4 let rammers sit within 100 px for most of each round.
- **MIR-1:** against a robot that drives to the reflection of our position through the
  field's centre, Hadur drives a path planned 110 ticks ahead and aims at the reflection of
  that path. 3.4's gun hit such robots about 8% of the time.
- Both stay off against the top 19: the gates are behaviour no top-19 robot showed in 81
  bench battles.
