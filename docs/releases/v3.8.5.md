Hadur 3.8.5 scopes the DrussGT route to the duels it was built for. Live, 3.8 gained against the top 10 and lost about 1.5 points a pairing against the bots ranked 21st and below ([live-3.8.md](../bench/live-3.8.md), [leak-38-plan.md](../leak-38-plan.md)).

- **The matchup gate (MATCH-1, MATCH-2).** Once both sides have fired 30 bullets and the hit rates clearly favour Hadur, the duel reads as won. The lower bound of Hadur's rate must sit above the enemy's upper bound. While the duel reads as won, Hadur aims without weighing shadows (GUN-7) and drops the one-bullet shielder latch (SHIELD-3). The rest of the battle is played as 3.7 played it. Against opponents that out-hit Hadur, which includes the whole top 10, nothing changes. Melee and team play are unchanged.
- **Released ahead of its bench gates.** No bench condition tried has reproduced 3.8's live loss (engine 1.9.5.6 and 1.11.1, slow CPU, each stage alone, a 300-opponent session), so the live pass is the measure. The gates against 3.8 on engine 1.11.1 (leak set, top 20, DrussGT) and 3.8 against 3.4 on the leak set run alongside it ([issue #117](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/issues/117)).
