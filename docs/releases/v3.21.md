# Hadur 3.21

Hadur 3.21 turns on the melee wave surfer. 1v1 and team play as 3.20. All three rumbles move to 3.21,
per the rule that minors move together.

- **Melee: 3.20 + MMOVE-15, the melee surfer** (PR #420 built it, PR #466 turned it on).
  - What it does: in melee off a team, every recorded enemy shot becomes a wave aimed at each robot
    it could have been fired at, weighted by the crowd (1/(1+k)^2 over the k nearer robots) and by
    the aims Hadur has learned from its own hits on that shooter. The least-risk candidates are
    re-ranked by the damage those waves would do on the drive there (TMODE-11's wave check), the
    way ScalarR, Neuromancer and Firestarter choose where to go.
  - pc-melee-surf (BENCH-136, PR #462) measured **+0.99 ± 0.19 APS** over the 3.19 melee on all
    46 census fields at 6 seeds (276 battles an arm, A/A +0.11 ± 0.21). Others outlived +3.0
    points, deaths with two opponents left -2.2 points, energy entering the endgame +3.6.
  - Live projection: about 68.7 against 3.20's 67.77 (bench gains have carried live about 1:1:
    3.18 was +1.2 on the bench and +1.52 live). 10th is Mirage at 68.22 and 9th Glacier at 68.84
    ([live read](../bench/live-3.20-melee.md)).
  - The ScalarR switches on top of the surfer (MGUN-22, MMOVE-16 to MMOVE-21) stay off; the card
    pc-melee-scal (BENCH-146) decides them for 3.22.
- **1v1: unchanged from 3.20.** The duel replay fixtures are bit for bit.
- **Team: unchanged from 3.20.** The team replay fixtures are bit for bit.
- **New version, new participant lines.** The package stays `hadur117`. The 3.20 lines come off.
- **PC clients stay off the live rumble.** Do not run `live-watch`, because the PC is not qualified
  (`QUALIFIED = no`).
