Hadur 2.1: the MeleeRumble entry.

**Download:** `hadur2.Hadur_2.1.jar` below. Robocode name `hadur2.Hadur 2.1`; runs on Java 11 or later.

What's in it:

- **Duel (one opponent left):** Hadur 2 through S2. Wave surfing and KNN guns with an anti-surfer array, an energy ledger that only turns real shots into waves, and a radar that finds the enemy again after a missed scan.
- **Melee (two or more opponents):** the 1.x melee brain, now inside the Hadur 2 core. It sweeps the radar toward whichever opponent was scanned longest ago and moves to the least risky nearby point. It shoots the cheapest kill with circular/linear aim and stays out of other robots' fights.

Melee bench, 10 robots, 1000x1000, 35 rounds per battle:

| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 sample bots | 1.0 | 23.0% |
| 9 established melee bots (Aleph, Tron, Coriantumr, ...) | 4.4 | 10.1% |
