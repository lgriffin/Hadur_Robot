Hadur 3.22 moves all three rumbles. In 1v1 it adds per-opponent lines from every card that held on the PC
since 3.21 (shield list adds, power rules, mimics, mirror-off) and the start-up stall fix. In melee it adds
the far-shot power rule. In the team it adds the close hunt and the last-enemy guard on every team, and the
close-quarters guard on NightmareTeam. All three rumbles move to 3.22, per the rule that minors move together.
