Hadur 3.8 is the DrussGT route (D1 to D5) and the first stage of the Team plan (T1). Against 3.7, paired on the same seeds, it scores **+10.5 ± 3.1 points against DrussGT** and **+7.2 ± 6.3 on the top-10 set** ([release check](../bench/release-check-3.8.md)).

- **Power by the lead (D1).** When both guns hit below break-even, Hadur fires the lightest bullet while level or ahead, and keeps a lead it would have given away. Never holds a shot it can pay for. Duress ends 300 ticks after the last skipped turn. Gate: DrussGT +12.0 against 3.7 ([d1-gate.md](../bench/d1-gate.md)).
- **A shield list (D5).** Against 14 opponents that the shield-mode probe showed it beats, Hadur opens each round still and shoots their bullets down, and leaves shield mode for the battle once it costs too much. Gate: +2.9 ± 2.7 points over a 46-robot panel ([d5-gate.md](../bench/d5-gate.md)).
- **Shield openers and the last shot (D2).** A shielder is caught on its first intercept and stored in the profile; the last shot that would hand the inactivity penalty to Hadur is kept ([d2-gate.md](../bench/d2-gate.md)).
- **Light bullets get their own aim, and bullets are armour (D3, D4).** Light shots have their own gun ratings and a sampled gun; the gun picks each angle for its hit chance plus the enemy bullets its shadow blocks on the path movement has planned. Stack gate against D1: top 10 +3.5 ± 3.2, weak set level ([stack-gate.md](../bench/stack-gate.md)).
- **Teammates stop running into each other (T1).** The team sees, predicts and steers around its own members, and the fire lane covers the bullet's whole flight. Gate against 3.7: teammate collisions 101,322 to 65, team score share 28.8% to 48.2% ([t1-team.md](../bench/t1-team.md)).
