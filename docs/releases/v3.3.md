Hadur 3.3: everything built since 3.2 in one version: rumble-safe memory (R5), the one-JVM
session bench and a duress mode for starved turns (R7), and movement that is harder to hit (R6).

**Download:** `hadur2.Hadur_3.3.jar`, or from the Google Drive link in the participant line at
the end. Robocode name `hadur2.Hadur 3.3`; runs on Java 11 or later. Enter it in both the
RoboRumble (1v1) and the MeleeRumble in place of 3.2: see
[docs/rumble-submission.md](https://github.com/lgriffin/Hadur_Robot/blob/master/docs/rumble-submission.md).

## Why one version

The plan had 3.3 (R5), 3.4 (R7) and 3.5 (R6). On 2026-10-01 the pace was too slow for three
live passes, so the three stages ship together; their gates that need long runs (A/B on the
top 10, the full session bisect) move to an end-of-plan pass. The weak set was checked
before each merge.

## What is new since 3.2

- **R5, rumble-safe memory** (MEM-8, MEM-9, MEM-10, ADAPT-4): stats-only profiles for most
  opponents (seeds only for the few met at least twice and scored under 60%), a per-format
  version prefix on profile names with carry-forward, at most one round-end checkpoint write.
  The data directory stays under 18 KB across 61 opponents.
- **R7, duress** (RES-9): after three skipped turns in a round the core stops learning and runs
  a cheap orbit at 400 px with head-on power-1.0 fire and a radar that searches when the
  enemy's position goes stale. Plus BENCH-6 and BENCH-7 (the 300-battle one-JVM session bench
  with a control robot) and RES-10 (live class count flat across a session).
- **R6, harder to hit** (MOVE-3, MOVE-4, MOVE-5, MOVE-7, PHYS-1, WAVE-4): the danger kernel is
  integrated over the precise intersection and the parts shadows let through, the flattener
  views switch on at 4.5%, the neighbour cache follows the wave, the predictor stops dead at
  a wall, and a corrected bullet power recomputes wall room. MOVE-6 is not in (needs a
  profile format change).

## What the session bench found on 3.2

300 battles in one JVM at the client's 512 MB heap: survival against weak bots stayed at 99
to 100% and the heap and live class count stayed flat, but **skipped turns per battle climbed
steadily from 10 to 90** while the control robot stayed at 0. That is Hadur's own per-tick cost
growing over a session, the likely mechanism of the live slide. Duress caps the damage; the
bisect (data directory wiped, fresh JVM per battle) is still running and will be written up
in the end-of-plan analysis.
