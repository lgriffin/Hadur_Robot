Hadur 3.7 completes the architecture evolution (stages A0 to A5). The solo robot plays as 3.6 does, and Hadur now has a team entry.

- **The World, one battlefield model.** The melee tracker is promoted to the kernel, and the conductor feeds it before any role. Only the battle's charter's roles are built, so a 1v1 carries no melee brain. The warm-up runs one throwaway tick per role, which fixes a warm-up fault that dated back to R1 (A3; [docs/bench/a3-melee.md](../bench/a3-melee.md)).
- **Ports for a team.** The adapter is now a `TeamRobot`. Messages, the bullet owner and a checksummed link codec are new, and each strand's store shelves are gated by charter. Old transcripts and profiles still load. The duel, melee and 300-battle session benches are level with A3 (A4; [docs/bench/a4-gate.md](../bench/a4-gate.md)).
- **The team baseline.** `hadur2.HadurTeam` is five Hadurs that share sightings and deaths, never target or count each other, and hold a shot while a teammate stands in the fire lane. On a team only the leader writes, and only its health record. Against eight teams there were no faults, no rejected messages, and no count of enemies below the truth (A5; [docs/bench/a5-team.md](../bench/a5-team.md)). This is a baseline for the team plan, not a team strategy.
