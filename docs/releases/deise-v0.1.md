Deise 0.1, a melee robot built to take MiniMelee 1st (docs/deise.md), released at stage E0 to get live numbers before the census and the fit.

- Saor's graded Markov gun, learning every robot it scans, not just its target.
- Range power capped at 3 and at what kills the target.
- Least-risk movement over a ring of 36 points. Opponents push by their energy, and Deise moves across the line of anyone likely aiming at it.
- Holds the corners while four or more opponents are left. Seen-shot waves are built but weigh nothing in this version.

Codesize 2,303 bytes. MiniMelee's limit is 1,499, so the rumble scores 0.1 in MeleeRumble only, not in the MiniMelee table. Its pairings against the MiniMelee robots still give its MiniMelee-shaped score. Stage E2 fits it to 1,499. On the cloud box it scored 55 to 58 APS in a top-ten field where Mirage 0.2 scored 63.
