Deise 0.2, a MiniMelee robot built to take 1st (docs/deise.md). It is 0.1's robot fitted into one class at 1,490 bytes, under MiniMelee's 1,499.

- Saor's graded Markov gun, learning every robot it scans, not just its target.
- Range power capped at 3 and at what kills the target.
- Least-risk movement over a ring of 36 points. Opponents push by their energy, and Deise moves across the line of anyone likely aiming at it.
- Holds the corners while four or more opponents are left.

The shipped class gives the same orders, to the bit, as the core-driven build it was flattened from (DEISE-16). On the cloud box it scored 55 to 58 APS in a top-ten field where Mirage 0.2 scored 63.

0.1 (2,303 bytes) is too big for MiniMelee and is not listed.
