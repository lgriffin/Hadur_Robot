Hadur 3.17 is a test release of the 1v1 mode table's next three stages, all per-opponent entries
benched against 3.16, and of the team's step C. It tests one assertion: that per-opponent table
entries carry over from the bench to live as table v1's did (live +9.5 points a robot on 3.16
against +12.7 on the bench). No global 1v1 switch changes from 3.16. Melee plays as 3.16.

- **1v1: shield part 2 (SHIELD-17, SHIELD-18 per opponent).** 16 robots join the shield list, 31
  get Saguaro's opening drive (the moving shield) and 221 get a shipped shield aim. Verify gate on
  fresh seeds over Saguaro's 300 shield robots: **+0.31 ± 0.12 APS** (A/A −0.02 ± 0.32; PR #256).
- **1v1: mode table v2, stop-and-go against surfers (POISON-1, POISON-2).** 18 WavePoison and 35
  WavePoisonShift entries. Verify gate: **+0.61 ± 0.10 APS** (PR #263).
- **1v1: shield part 3, warm shield and stop-and-go pass 2.** 11 more robots join the shield list
  (8 with the opening drive, 11 shipped aims), plus 8 WavePoison and 12 WavePoisonShift entries.
  Verify gate on 31 robots, 4 fresh seeds: **+0.11 ± 0.03 APS** (A/A +0.36 ± 0.82; PR #276).
- **1v1: no simple-aim dodge entries.** The dodge mode locked no robot (table v2), so it ships off.
- **Team: step C, Ogi's narrow fire lane with MATEFIRE (WEAVE-9, TMODE-12).** Team gate over 46
  teams x 6 seeds against 3.16: +2.9 ± 0.9 points (A/A +0.6 ± 0.7; PR #264).
- **Off, as in 3.16:** the stop-point surf and its gun ensemble (MOVE-25 to MOVE-27), the simple-aim
  dodge (DODGE-1 to DODGE-3), the WAVES and HUNT team modes and the radar look (MRADAR-3).
- **Live expectation.** The three 1v1 stages sum to about +1.03 APS on the bench (lower ends
  +0.78). At table v1's transfer of about 75%, 3.16's live 89.25 projects to about **90.0**, with a
  lower end of about 89.8. That straddles the 90 release rule rather than clearing it, so read
  3.17 live by opponent group (table robots against the rest) after its first full pass.
