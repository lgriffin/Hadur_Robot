Hadur 3.18 is a trial release across all three rumbles. Melee carries two census-gated changes. 1v1
plays exactly as 3.17 but makes a third less garbage, a test of whether fewer runtime pauses lower
the skipped turns that pile up on long-running clients. Team plays as 3.17.1.

- **1v1 (and the shared core): a third less garbage, bit for bit (TIME-9, PR #343).**
  - Replaying 15 battles, allocation falls from 19.96 to 13.34 GB (-33%). Every duel, melee and
    team replay fixture replays bit for bit.
  - Why: in the 3.17 release watch (PR #331), PC clients left running climbed from 4 to 87 skipped
    turns a battle in 75 minutes, and Hadur's share fell from +0.4 over its live pairings to -3.9
    under them.
  - Profiling found no leak and no stuck thread. The stalls are runtime pauses landing in Hadur's
    turns, and Hadur made 58% of the garbage behind them.
  - No bench can see this gain, so the live read is the test.
- **Melee: banked gun memory and the two-left endgame (MGUN-7, MEND-3; PR #322, #325).** The
  melee field gun keeps 24 situations per opponent, up from 12, off a team. With two opponents
  left, Hadur avoids being either one's closest robot. Census over the 46-field set at 8 fresh
  seeds: **+0.62 ± 0.14 APS**.
- **Melee: Ogi's crowd power (MGUN-13; PR #330, #334).** Power 3.0 within 200 px and 1.92
  beyond, off a team with three or more opponents and 20+ energy. **+0.57 ± 0.18 APS** on top.
- **Team: as 3.17.1.** The new team switches (LINES, LINESW, LANEP, LANEB, GUNLINE) ship off and
  the per-team table is empty, so HadurTeam plays as 3.17.1 apart from TIME-9's timing.
- **Live expectation.**
  - 1v1: 3.17 reads 90.91. Any change comes from fewer skipped turns. If the cut helps, the
    gain is up to about +0.4 (the fresh-client margin), and probably less.
  - Melee: census about 68.2, which projects to about **67.4** live against 3.17's 66.40.
  - Team: 3.17.1's 79.39, within noise.
- **Release watch.** Run `.\queue.ps1 run live-watch --fresh` at release (BENCH-113, BENCH-121).
  It starts all eight PC clients itself (close hand-started ones first), restarts each every 15
  minutes between battles, and reports skipped turns and share by client uptime.
