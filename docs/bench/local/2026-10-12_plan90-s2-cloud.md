# Plan-90 step 3, cloud sweep: the step-1 shield on the 198 robots only Nullstride shields

**Result: shielding all 198 with the step-1 winner's shield (`3.12shslsmsssa`: no attack shots,
learning from intercepts, the most frequent offset, no in-round exits, every opponent listed)
scores +6.2 points a bot (+4.8 to +7.6), +1.00 APS over the field, before any robot is picked.
136 of 198 go up. By the D5 rule at 3 seeds, 82 robots qualify, worth +1.05 APS on this
reading.** That figure is optimistic, because the same seeds chose them. The PC confirms at 8
seeds (`plans/plan90.queue` step `s2`).

Run: 2026-10-11 23:23 to 2026-10-12 01:56 UTC, cloud container (4 cores, `--parallel 3`, cpu
constant 4138771, engine 1.11.1, 35 rounds, cold, seeds 901-903). Set `shield-ns-sweep.txt`
(Nullstride's list less Hadur's, PR #190). Baseline: the released 3.12 jar. A container restart
stopped the baseline after 418 battles; the 59 robots it had not finished were rerun whole
(`s2-3.12b`) and merged in, and 2 battles have no result. Rows:
`data/bench/2026-10-12_hadur-plan90-s2-cloud_cold.tsv` (and `_rounds.tsv`); per robot:
`data/bench/2026-10-12_hadur-plan90-s2-cloud_perbot.tsv` (mean difference, the t interval's
lower end at 3 seeds, the D5 pick).
