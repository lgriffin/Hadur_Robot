# M0: sizing the 3.11 census instrument

Date: 2026-10-08. Stage M0 of the 3.11 census plan (artifact "Hadur Gap to Top 10"). Bench tooling, set files and data only; no change to `hadur-core` or `hadur-robot`, nothing released.

## What was run

- Census set: `hadur-bench/census-311.txt`, 1215 opponents, every participant in the 2026-10-08T1238Z 1v1 rankings (`data/rumble/parsed/2026-10-08T1238Z_roborumble_rankings.tsv`) less Hadur. Each line carries live rank and band. Bands: 1 = ranks 1 to 10 (10 bots), 2 = 11 to 50 (39), 3 = 51 to 200 (150), 4 = 201 to 400 (200), 5 = 401 to 700 (300), 6 = 701 and below (516). All 1215 archive jars are fetched into `hadur-bench/opponents/` (470 downloaded, 745 already present, 0 failed).
- Timing sample: `hadur-bench/m0-timing.txt`, 8 opponents drawn at random from each band (`random.seed(311)`), 48 battles. Hadur 3.10 release jar, 1 seed, 35 rounds, Robocode 1.11.1, parallel 12, child-cpus 2, child heap 2G, CPU constant 1488498. All RoboRumble workers stopped; `jps -l` showed no other JVM before the start.
- Whole run: 133 s wall clock for 48 battles, including start-up and a 125 s battle that ran alone at the end.
- Rows: `data/bench/2026-10-08_hadur-m0-timing-local_cold.tsv` (48) and `..._rounds.tsv` (1680, 48 x 35). Report and per-opponent files: `docs/bench/local/2026-10-08_m0-timing*`.

## M1 size

| Plan | Battles | Hours at parallel 12 | 95% range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.10 and Tomcat 3.68, 4 seeds | 9,720 | 2.5 | 2.2 to 2.7 |
| Add Nullstride, 4 seeds | 14,580 | 3.7 | 3.3 to 4.1 |
| One subject, 4 seeds | 4,860 | 1.2 | 1.1 to 1.4 |

That is about 4,000 battles an hour across the census mix, against 1.2 a second (4,300 an hour) for the 48-battle sample, which is top-heavy by design. M1 with Nullstride fits a night with room. Assumptions that could move it: the subject changes battle length (the sample is 3.10 only, and the figure for Tomcat or Nullstride as subject is not measured); Tomcat does not fight itself, so that pass has 1214 battles; the 8-a-band sample is small, so the range above is the sampling error only.

Disk: 2.0 MB a battle in the work directory, so 20 GB for 9,720 battles and 30 GB for 14,580 (D: has 9.7 TB free). The rows file would be about 400 KB; a rounds file for 9,720 battles would be about 30 MB, so M1 should not commit one unless it is wanted.

G0 on its own terms is met: no denial, no other Robocode JVM.

Duress. In 9 of the 10 duress battles the count is exactly 298 ticks, all in round 0, and the tenth is 386 ticks, also round 0. A fixed count in the first round looks like start-up warm-up, not load, but that is my reading from the rows, not something tested. If the plan's rule "drop duress rows" were applied literally, 10 of 48 rows (21%) would go. Recommendation: for M1 keep round-0 start-up duress, drop battles with duress after round 0 (none here), and report the dropped count. That is the owner's call.

The earlier `otherJvms=1` readings (122 of 480 flat-311 gungap battles, 62 of 312 tail battles) did not recur: 0 of 48 here with the host idle and a single bench invocation. I have not found their cause. M1 records the column on every row, so a recurrence will show, and rows with `otherJvms > 0` should be dropped and counted, as the plan says.

Loadability. All 48 sampled opponents loaded and finished 35 rounds; with 0 failures in 48 the 95% upper bound on the census failure rate is about 6%. The other 1167 bots have not been probed for loadability, so M1 should expect and report some failing opponents under "Excluded or failing opponents".
