## 4. Why the PC disagreed: the turn time

Both robots had lost this line on the PC before: Musashi -3.0 in pc-shield-audit and Parallax -0.2 in
pc-still-field, each on 12 seeds. On the cloud, the bench calibrates its CPU constant (2,447,952 ns for the lock; it drifts with the box's load). The PC
cards pin 1,488,498 ns and run with 16 workers. Rerunning the same seeds at 1,488,498 on the cloud:

- Musashi mtzl: +20.3 becomes +2.7 +- 5.7. Parallax: +5.7 becomes +3.7 +- 3.9. That matches the PC's verdicts.
- Hadur skips about 13 turns a battle mid-round in shield mode, against 2.3 at the calibrated constant. The skips
  are spread evenly through the round, not at start-up.
- The shield works in rounds 1-4 (4.3 damage taken a round), then leaks (16.8). Every Musashi battle leaves
  shield mode for the battle on the `damage` exit between rounds 13 and 17. At the calibrated constant, it never
  leaves against Musashi.
- With the exit turned off (sx0list), Musashi is -5.7 +- 3.4. The leaks grow to 48 damage a round by rounds
  15-19, because the enemy's gun learns the gaps a skipped turn opens.

Paired by robot, the tight constant costs BeepBoop 4.47 +- 2.66 points and leaves us level (+1.65 +- 2.68). The
calibrated constant reproduces live on these robots; the tight one does not. A PC card at 1,488,498 with 16
workers therefore undercounts what CPU-heavy play earns live, shield lines among it. The pc-r31 card now runs at
3,272,413, the calibrated value of the run that matched live (the PC's skips at 1,488,498 match the cloud's at
that value: 26.8 a battle in pc-shield-audit against 29 here, so the same constant should match on the PC too).

  ColorNanoP, PointInLineRRAL, NeophytePRAL, Monte) still lose at the calibrated timing (all negative, 4 to 9

- The 9 unlisted robots BeepBoop scores 99+ against live (Booring, AspidReloaded, TopGun, Ihivatar, VirginSteele,
  Oranges, Twardy, MoxieBot, Pegasus): none holds the shield line on 12 seeds (best AspidReloaded +2.8, low
  end -4.0; five lose 7 to 19 points).
- A second 12 seeds (21500): Parallax +2.4. Over 24 seeds it is +4.03 +- 2.65 (low end +1.38) and holds.
  AspidReloaded (+3.07, low end -0.69) and SquirmyToad (+1.84, low end -0.18) do not.
- Musashi does not reproduce. The merged master build on 8 new seeds gives +8.5 +- 4.7. On the lock's own 12 seeds
  at 2,397,234 ns (pinned, close to the lock's calibrated 2,447,952) it gives +2.2 +- 4.0. The lock's own build, rebuilt and rerun on those seeds at the same constant,
  gives +2.95 +- 3.60. The difference is the box: the lock ran while we skipped about 4.5 turns a battle, the
  reruns while we skipped 10 to 13, and the shield leaks and exits as in section 4. The cloud box's effective
  speed varies through the night even at a pinned constant. Musashi's line is worth +20 when we rarely skip and
  +2 to +3 when we skip about 10 times a battle.

## Outcome

- Nothing banked. Parallax held +4.03 +- 2.65 over 24 seeds (low end +1.38), but its two 12-seed halves read +5.7
  and +2.4, and A/A pairs of one build on the cloud this night differ by 2.2 (mu, 12 seeds) and 5.3 points (mv,
  8 seeds). That is inside the box's drift. Musashi's lock collapsed on rerun. Both robots are in pc-r31's
  shield arm, which decides them on the PC at one constant.
- Not banked: the 9 failed leads; SquirmyToad and AspidReloaded (low end below 0 over 24 seeds: fam or bs9 plus cf3).
- Constants: the cloud calibrates per run and drifted from 2,447,952 (lk) to 3,091,257 (cf3), 3,247,780 (bs9),
  3,272,413 (cc-cl) and 3,425,931 (mv). Only cpu, cpu2, cc-pc (1,488,498) and mu, mu2 (2,397,234) were pinned.
- Lead: our skipped turns. Each one can let a shot through a shield, and the enemy gun learns from it. A shield that
  does not skip (BeepBoop scores 98.1 against Musashi live) is worth about 20 points on Musashi alone.
