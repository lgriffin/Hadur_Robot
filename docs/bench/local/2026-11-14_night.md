## 4. Why the PC disagreed: the turn time

Both robots had lost this line on the PC before: Musashi -3.0 in pc-shield-audit and Parallax -0.2 in
pc-still-field, each on 12 seeds. On the cloud, the bench calibrates its CPU constant (2,397,234 ns here). The PC
cards pin 1,488,498 ns and run with 16 workers. Rerunning the same seeds at 1,488,498 on the cloud:

- Musashi mtzl: +20.3 becomes +2.7 +- 5.7. Parallax: +5.7 becomes +3.7 +- 3.9. That matches the PC's verdicts.
- Hadur skips about 13 turns a battle mid-round in shield mode, against 2.3 at the calibrated constant. The skips
  are spread evenly through the round, not at start-up.
- The shield works in rounds 1-4 (4.3 damage taken a round), then leaks (16.8). Every Musashi battle leaves
  shield mode for the battle on the `damage` exit between rounds 13 and 17. At the calibrated constant, it never
  leaves against Musashi.
- With the exit turned off (sx0list), Musashi is -5.7 +- 3.4. The leaks grow to 48 damage a round by rounds
  15-19, because the enemy's gun learns the gaps a skipped turn opens.

## 5. Which timing matches live? 31 robots x c / BeepBoop 2.0 x both constants (cc-pc, cc-cl, seed 21200)

| Timing | Our skips a battle | BeepBoop's skips a battle | Our score | BeepBoop's score | Gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1,488,498 ns (the PC cards' value) | 29 | 496 | 70.2 | 79.8 | +9.65 +- 3.29 |
| calibrated, 2,397,234 ns | 6 | 35 | 68.5 | 84.3 | +15.76 +- 2.72 |
| live LiteRumble, 13 Nov | - | - | 66.2 | 83.6 | +17.4 |

Paired by robot, the tight constant costs BeepBoop 4.47 +- 2.66 points and leaves us level (+1.65 +- 2.68). The
calibrated constant reproduces live on these robots; the tight one does not. A PC card at 1,488,498 with 16
workers therefore undercounts what CPU-heavy play earns live, shield lines among it. The pc-r31 card now runs at
2,397,234 (the PC's skips at 1,488,498 match the cloud's at that value: 26.8 a battle in pc-shield-audit against
29 here).

## Outcome

- Banked: Musashi and Parallax get the shield list and still lines (12 fresh seeds, low end above 0, at the
  live-like timing).
- Not banked: the 9 failed leads; SquirmyToad (low end +0.07, too thin).
- Lead: our skipped turns. Each one can let a shot through a shield, and the enemy gun learns from it.
