# Top 3 stage 0, first read: where the bullets that hit Hadur come from

2026-10-15. Hadur 3.13 with MOVE-9's HF record (no behaviour change), on the 400-bot screen
(`hadur-bench/screen-311.txt`) at 2 seeds (201, 202), in the cloud: 4 cores, parallel 3, CPU
constant 4138771, 5.0 skipped turns a battle. Run report: `2026-10-15_top3-s0-hf.md`. Read with
`python3 data/tools/hit_forensics.py WORKDIR --set hadur-bench/screen-311.txt`, which leaves out
the robots on Hadur's and Nullstride's shield lists (302 of the 400 remain) and counts only the
hits taken while the Duel drove.

## What it says

- **Detection is not the gap.** Bullets with no wave are 0.1 a round, under 1% of the damage.
- **The views mostly knew.** Only 13% of the damage (1.7 of 13.0 a round) came from angles the
  views rated safer than two-thirds of the wave. A third (4.6) came from angles they rated among
  the most dangerous third, and the decile at the very top holds more than any other below rank
  400 (1.48 a round of 8.5). The model is not what fails. The surf ends up where it knows the
  danger is.
- **Simple guns land a third of it.** Head-on and linear aims are 4.2 of 13.0 a round, 3.0 of
  8.5 from ranks 401+. Three-quarters of those were rated mid or known.
- **Close range and heavy bullets, below rank 400.** Of the 8.5 a round from ranks 401+, 3.9 came
  from bullets fired within 350 px (1.7 within 200 px), and 4.5 from bullets of power 2.5 or more.

So lever 1 is about reach and choice, not learning or detection: either no reachable option was
safe (too close for the bullet's flight time, or hemmed in by a wall), or a safer one was
reachable and the surf picked another (the distancing factor, the second wave, the stop option).
