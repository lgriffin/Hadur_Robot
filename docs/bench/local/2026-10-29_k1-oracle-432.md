# Gun oracle: every model scored on the same bullets

Data: `oracle-k1.npz`, 779545 enemy bullets matched to the wave the surf rated, 432 opponents. Means per opponent, each opponent weighted equally. **bits**: log2 of the model's density at the bullet's guess factor over the uniform's, after a 5% uniform mix (higher is better). **safe**: the share of bullets from the third of the wave the model rated least dangerous, the third the surf drives into (lower is better; 0.333 knows nothing). **calibrated**: bits after the further uniform mix (in brackets) that scores best, which takes out overconfidence, so models compare on what they know. **rank**: the share of the wave the model rated safer than where the bullet went (0.5 knows nothing, higher is better); safe and rank read only the model's ranking, which is what the surf acts on. **coverage**: the share of bullets on which the model had a surface (a view off or empty has none).
