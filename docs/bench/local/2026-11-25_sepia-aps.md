# Sepia: an estimate of its 1v1 APS (BENCH-294)

2026-11-25, on master at 49b2df35. No battle was played: Sepia ([docs/sepia-design.md](../../sepia-design.md)) is a
proposal, and this is a model of it built from battles Hadur has already fought. Run with

    python3 data/tools/sepia_aps.py --jobs 2

which writes one row per robot to [sepia-aps.tsv](sepia-aps.tsv) and every number below to
[sepia-aps.json](sepia-aps.json). It takes about seven minutes and reproduces both files exactly.

## The estimate

**Sepia as designed: 86.70 APS, 17th of 1,213.** Over 4,000 draws of the assumptions the 10th, 50th and 90th
percentiles are 84.70, 86.25 and 87.53 (26th, 22nd and 14th). Hadur 3.24 is at 93.44 live and 10th place at 87.64.
The chance of 87.64 or more is 8%, and of the design doc's 88 gate 4%.

| Each row changes one thing | APS |
|---|---|
| Hadur 3.24, live | 93.44 |
| Hadur 3.13's play on the robots it does not shield today, 3.24's score on the shield list | 91.28 |
| Sepia, if its gait were hit as Hadur's surf is | 90.00 |
| Sepia, hit 1.21 times as often by strong guns, with no floors | 88.81 |
| **Sepia as designed: the floors too** | **86.70** |

Two variants of the last row: punching only, with no ink at all, 86.58; and with ink that stops what the cloud
model says (67%, not 49%), 87.37.

## How it is built

1. **A round model.** Both robots start at 100. The enemy fires as often and as hard as it did against Hadur in
   the 3.13 census (8 battles of 35 rounds against each of 1,212 robots), hits with a given chance and gets 3 x
   power back on a hit. We fire as Hadur did, for the share of our gun time that goes to punches. The round ends
   when one side is out of energy and is scored as Robocode scores it. 200 rounds a robot, by Monte Carlo.
2. **The check.** With Hadur's own hit rates the model gives 89.95 against a measured 89.66 over 1,192 robots: a
   mean miss of 1.15 points, 82% of robots within 2 and 96% within 5, r = 0.98. It is weakest on the 76 robots
   Hadur scored under 70% against (mean miss 4.2). Each robot's miss is added back to its projection.
3. **The grid.** Projections are read off a grid of 15 hit chances from 0 to 95%, the most any gun is given. Read
   at Hadur's own hit rates the grid gives back the direct play: 0.00 off on average over the 894 robots it
   covers, and 0.24 off on the 21 that hit Hadur over 45% of the time. A hit chance outside the grid is an error.
4. **Shielded robots.** On the 298 robots of today's shield list that are in both tables, Sepia plays Hadur's
   shield and takes Hadur 3.24's live score less 0.7 (0 to 2.0).
5. **Every other robot** is played with the hit chance Sepia's gait would give its gun, in four regimes (punch
   only; alternate; one ink per enemy shot, then punch; ink only), and the best is taken.

The hit chance on Sepia, for a robot whose true hit rate on Hadur is h. The census read every enemy shot's aim
at birth, so it knows the share `aimed` of a robot's bullets that a head-on, linear or circular aim explains: 46%
over the 894 robots. Hadur's forensics class the bullets that hit it by where they landed, which gives `sigma`,
the share of its hits that simple-aimed bullets made: 45%.

| Bullets | Sepia punching | Sepia inking |
|---|---|---|
| A simple aim explains them | the larger of 1.15 sigma h and what the exact pick let through | the same |
| No simple aim explains them, on the body | the larger of 1.21 (1 - sigma) h and (1 - aimed) spray floors | the larger of 1.63 (1 - sigma) h and 1.5 (1 - aimed) spray floors |
| No simple aim explains them, stopped by ink | none | 49% of those on the body, less in the mixed regimes |

- **1.21 and 1.63** set the lab against the cloud model. The movement lab measured its GFHist and Knn shooters at
  9.3% and 13.3% on silent Hadur ([2026-10-16_lab-first-read.md](2026-10-16_lab-first-read.md)). The gait runs
  (`data/tools/ink_model/e6.json`) put a census-grade guess-factor gun on the body 12.7% of the time against the
  two-way lottery and 8.8% against the least-exposed pick, and a segmented gun 20.0% and 13.7%: four ratios, 0.95
  to 1.50, mean 1.21. Inking, the lottery gives 15.2% and 21.5%.
- **The spray floor** is what an even spread of bullets over the escape angle would hit: a moving body's 52 px
  over 2 x asin(8 / bullet speed) x 450 px, 9.5% at power 2. A surfer is hit less than that by a gun it can read;
  a gait that commits one of three paths a wave is not, unless the aim is simple. The weakest gun in the gait runs
  gets 0.93 of it against the pick and 1.34 against the lottery. It is owed on the bullets no simple aim explains,
  so it is weighted by their share of the bullets fired. It binds on 79% of the 894 robots: over those 894,
  bullets hit Hadur 6.5% of the time and a punching Sepia 10.5%.
- **What the exact pick let through.** A simple aim Sepia can reproduce is dodged by taking the option its bullet
  misses. In the gait runs that pick leaves a head-on gun on the body 0.2% of the time and a linear gun 1.8%
  (the lab's HeadOn, Linear and Circular shooters hit silent Hadur 1.0%, 0.5% and 2.5%). Those rates, by the share
  of a robot's bullets so aimed, are the least its simple aims get. It binds on 12% of the 894.
- **sigma and circular aims.** Hadur's forensics have a head-on and a linear class and no circular one, so a
  circular-aimed bullet that hit is among the hits they could class as neither, unless it landed on the linear
  aim. `circ` is how likely a circular-aimed bullet is to have made one of those hits against a bullet no simple
  aim explains: 0 gives it none, 1 its share by count, and 0.5 is central. Under 20 classed hits (108 of the 894
  robots) sigma is `aimed`.
- **49%** is the cloud model's 67% against aimed guns, scaled by the Q0 gate over the model's cover (35 / 48.3).

**Rammers.** The 87 robots the census classes as rammers score 77.84 against Sepia, where Hadur 3.13 had 87.66 and

the `close` row and 19 of the 65 of the `linear` row. Without them the `linear` row is 95.95, against Hadur

**Regimes taken** on the 894: punch only 870, ink only 21, ink first 2, alternate 1. Ink only is best against

Hadur 3.24 has 25.6% live, provided BeepBoop keeps firing as it fired at Hadur. Everywhere else a punch is worth
more, because the damage takes energy the enemy would otherwise fire. Over the field the ink adds 0.11 APS.

With every assumption at its worse end the estimate is 80.44, and at its better end 91.19.

## What it says

- **About 17th as designed, and the top 10 is roughly 1 chance in 12.**
- **The gap is in the punching, not the inking.** Of the 6.7 points to Hadur 3.24, 2.2 are what Hadur has gained
  since 3.13 on the robots it does not shield (mostly its per-robot table), 1.3 the allowances for a new robot
  (its gun, the shield, simple aims and rammers), and 3.3 the jet cycle being hit while Sepia punches: 1.2 from
  strong guns and 2.1 from the floors.
- **Ink is a specialist's tool.** It is in the best regime against 24 robots and worth 0.1 to 0.8 APS over the
  field.
- **Rammers cost about half a point.** With a rammer hitting Sepia no more often than it hits Hadur the estimate
  is 87.19. An escape is a Q5 card in the design.
- **The number to measure first** is damage taken by silent Sepia with its ink off against the lab shooters, set
  against silent Hadur's. The `cloud-ink-lab` card (BENCH-295) carries that arm and a gate on it.

## What the model cannot see

- Whether an opponent changes how it fires at a robot that does not shoot back. The ink-only rows assume not.
- Today's gun. The base is Hadur 3.13's hit rate and damage on each robot; 3.24's gun is no worse.
- A robot Hadur hardly shot at. Sepia fires as Hadur did, so against the three robots Hadur fired under five
  shots a round at it fires as little, however often it is hit. Wolverine 2.1 is the one this hurts: 46.2%, where
  Hadur 3.13 had 91.2%.
- The engine. The gait runs are geometry on a straight track with no walls, and their guns are the census's
  simple ones. The exact pick knows the bullet's line exactly; a real mimic is a tick or a variant off at times.
- Robots new since the census: 16 of the live table's 1,208 have no census row and take the mean of the robots
  Sepia does not shield.
