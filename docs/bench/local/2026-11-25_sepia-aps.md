# Sepia: an estimate of its 1v1 APS (BENCH-294)

2026-11-25, on master at 49b2df35. No battle was played: Sepia ([docs/sepia-design.md](../../sepia-design.md)) is a
proposal, and this is a model of it built from battles Hadur has already fought. Run with

    python3 data/tools/sepia_aps.py --jobs 2

which writes one row per robot to [sepia-aps.tsv](sepia-aps.tsv) and every number below to
[sepia-aps.json](sepia-aps.json). It takes about five minutes and reproduces both files exactly.

## The estimate

**Sepia as designed: 86.65 APS, 17th of 1,213.** Over 4,000 draws of the assumptions the 10th, 50th and 90th
percentiles are 84.62, 86.29 and 87.61 (27th, 21st and 11th). Hadur 3.24 is at 93.44 live and 10th place at 87.64.
The chance of 87.64 or more is 9%, and of the design doc's 88 gate 4%.

|---|---|
| Hadur 3.24, live | 93.44 |
| Hadur 3.13's play on the robots it does not shield today, 3.24's score on the shield list | 91.28 |
| Sepia, if its gait were hit as Hadur's surf is | 90.49 |
| Sepia, hit 1.26 times as often by strong guns, with no floor for weak ones | 89.01 |

Two variants of the last row: punching only, with no ink at all, 86.52; and with ink that stops what the cloud
model says (67%, not 49%), 87.41.

## How it is built

1. **A round model.** Both robots start at 100. The enemy fires as often and as hard as it did against Hadur in
   the 3.13 census (8 battles of 35 rounds against each of 1,212 robots), hits with a given chance and gets 3 x
   power back on a hit. We fire as Hadur did, for the share of our gun time that goes to punches. The round ends
   when one side is out of energy and is scored as Robocode scores it. 200 rounds a robot, by Monte Carlo.
2. **The check.** With Hadur's own hit rates the model gives 89.95 against a measured 89.66 over 1,192 robots: a
   mean miss of 1.15 points, 82% of robots within 2 and 96% within 5, r = 0.98. It is weakest on the 76 robots
   Hadur scored under 70% against (mean miss 4.2). Each robot's miss is added back to its projection.
3. **Shielded robots.** On the 298 robots of today's shield list that are in both tables, Sepia plays Hadur's
   shield and takes Hadur 3.24's live score less 0.7 (0 to 2.0).
4. **Every other robot** is played with the hit chance Sepia's gait would give its gun, in four regimes (punch
   only; alternate; one ink per enemy shot, then punch; ink only), and the best is taken.

The hit chance on Sepia, for a robot whose true hit rate on Hadur is h:

| Bullets | Sepia punching | Sepia inking |
|---|---|---|
| Explained by a simple aim (head-on, linear, circular) | 1.15 h | 1.15 h |
| All others, on the body | the larger of 1.26 h and the spray floor | the larger of 1.63 h and 1.5 floors |
| All others, stopped by ink | none | 49% of those on the body, less in the mixed regimes |

- **1.26 and 1.63** set the lab against the cloud model. The movement lab measured its GFHist and Knn shooters at
  9.3% and 13.3% on silent Hadur ([2026-10-16_lab-first-read.md](2026-10-16_lab-first-read.md)). The gait runs
  (`data/tools/ink_model/e6.json`) put a census-grade guess-factor gun on the body 12.7% of the time against the
  two-way lottery and 9.3% against the least-exposed pick, and a segmented gun 20.0% and 15.6%: four ratios, 1.00
  to 1.50, mean 1.26. Inking, the lottery gives 15.2% and 21.5%.
- **The spray floor** is what an even spread of bullets over the escape angle would hit: a moving body's 52 px
  over 2 x asin(8 / bullet speed) x 450 px, 9.5% at power 2. A surfer is hit less than that by a gun it can read;
  a gait that commits one of three paths a wave is not, unless the aim is simple. The weakest gun in the gait runs
  gets 0.98 of the floor against the pick and 1.34 against the lottery. The floor binds on 76% of the 894 robots
  Sepia does not shield: over those 894, bullets hit Hadur 6.5% of the time and a punching Sepia 10.9%.
- **49%** is the cloud model's 67% against aimed guns, scaled by the Q0 gate over the model's cover (35 / 48.3).

| `k_ink`: strong guns on the inking gait | 1.38 | 1.63 | 1.87 | 86.65 | 86.62 |

With every assumption at its worse end the estimate is 80.89, and at its better end 90.81.

## What it says

- **About 17th as designed, and the top 10 is roughly 1 chance in 11.**
- **The gap is in the punching, not the inking.** Of the 6.8 points to Hadur 3.24, 2.2 are what Hadur has gained
  since 3.13 on the robots it does not shield (mostly its per-robot table), 0.8 the allowances for a new robot, and
  3.8 the jet cycle being hit while Sepia punches: 1.5 from strong guns and 2.4 from the floor for weak ones.
- **Ink is a specialist's tool.** It is the best regime against 22 robots and worth 0.1 to 0.9 APS over the field.
- **The number to measure first** is damage taken by silent Sepia with its ink off against the lab shooters, set
  against silent Hadur's. The `cloud-ink-lab` card (BENCH-295) now carries that arm and a gate on it.

## What the model cannot see

- Whether an opponent changes how it fires at a robot that does not shoot back. The ink-only rows assume not.
- Today's gun. The base is Hadur 3.13's hit rate and damage on each robot; 3.24's gun is no worse.
- The engine. The gait runs are geometry on a straight track, and their guns are the census's simple ones.
- Robots new since the census: 16 of the live table's 1,208 have no census row and take the mean of the robots
  Sepia does not shield.
