# Plan-90 on the PC: steps 1, 3 and 5 read against the gates, and the 3.13 candidate

**Result: the 3.13 candidate ships shield switches hold, learn, mode and stay (SHIELD-13), and
103 more robots on the shield list. The PC's 8 seeds put it at about +1.4 APS on the bench over
3.12:**
- **+0.29** on the 89 robots both lists already shielded.
- **+1.11** on the 103 robots it adds. That figure is read on the cloud's separate seeds, so it
  carries no bias from the seeds that chose the robots.

If live keeps 70% of the bench gain, as it did for 3.10's list, that is about +1.0 APS live. The
4 switches tie with one another, so 3.13 ships the build step 3 measured. Before release, the PC
gate `plans/plan90-gate.queue` runs 3.13 against 3.12 on fresh seeds.

Runs: PR #191. Leigh's PC (Threadripper PRO 9965WX, `--parallel 12`, CPU constant 1488498,
engine 1.11.1, 35 rounds, cold), 2026-10-12. The rows are in
`data/bench/2026-10-12_hadur-plan90-*-local_cold.tsv`, and each build is paired with the released
3.12 at the same seed (911-918). APS is the sum of the per-robot differences over 1,217 pairings.

- **Holding fire is the whole step-1 gain,** as on the cloud: about +4 points on these robots.
- **Learn, mode and turn do nothing measurable.** The cloud's 3 seeds read stay as +1.3. Here,
  at 8 seeds, it reads level. Its spread is wide (±1.4), because it moves points between robots:
  band 3 gains about 1.4 and band 4 loses about 1.6.
- **So there is no switch to choose between on step 1.** 3.13 ships the build that step 3 swept
  with (hold, learn, mode, stay), so the list and the design were measured together. Turn stays
  off. It adds nothing, and it is a reconstruction of Nullstride's method, not a copy.
