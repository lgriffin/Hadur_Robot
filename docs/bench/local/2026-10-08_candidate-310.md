## The top 20 (gate 2)

No top-20 robot is on the list, so the expected difference is 0. The measured -0.82 has an interval of [-2.69, +1.06], which includes 0 but also does not exclude a loss of a couple of points, and the equivalence margin of 1.0 is not met (not resolved). Five seeds over 20 robots and 22% untrusted pairs cannot settle it. No robot is significantly different (Holm). The lowest nominal differences are xander.cat.XanderCat 12.9 -7.1 +/- 9.4, voidious.Diamond 1.8.22 -6.3 +/- 8.2, jk.melee.Neuromancer 7.12 -5.4 +/- 8.9. If the owner wants this closed, the follow-up is more seeds on the top 20; 3.10 should play those robots exactly as 3.9 does, so a real effect would be a surprise.

## The tail (gate 3)

-0.13 [-0.65, +0.39], level. Nothing moved.

## Caveats

- Every step is gate CAUTION (11 to 24% of pairs untrusted, equal load on both sides, one other Robocode JVM at most). Fine for gates of this size, not for claims of a fraction of a point.
- The list gain is against the same DrussGT-list robots the sweep chose from. It says nothing about robots off the list.
- A shield-list entry matches one exact version, so a live opponent that updates its version leaves the list. That is outside what this bench measures.
