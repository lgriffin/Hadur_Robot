# Carried-over lines on two robots' new versions (cloud, seeds 16900 and 17000)

SHIELD-26 (PR #794) makes every per-opponent line match its robot by name, so three lines
benched on an older version came back to life: DrussGT 3.1.16's heat-jit line (live 3.1.18)
and Seraphim 2.3.1's shield entry and shield aim (live 2.4.0). This run benches 3.22 without
them (r22) against 3.22 with them matched to the live versions (vr), 35 rounds, 16 seeds a robot
(8 from 16900, 8 from 17000).

| Robot | Seeds | vr - r22 (points) | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| cs.s2.Seraphim 2.4.0 | 16 | -5.04 | ± 3.76 |
| jk.mega.DrussGT 3.1.18 | 16 | +0.31 | ± 3.97 |

Seraphim's lines lose on 2.4.0 on both halves (-5.78, then -4.31) and leave the tables: the
shield entry in `ShieldListData` and the aim in `ShieldTableData.aims()`. DrussGT's heat-jit line
is flat on 3.1.18 and stays (a line carries over unless a bench shows it loses).
