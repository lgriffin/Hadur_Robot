## Reading

- **Attack shots were the largest single leak, as the plan's simulation said.** Holding fire
  alone is +4.2 points a bot. 75 of the 89 robots go up.
- **Learning from intercepts and the most frequent offset add +0.4.** That is inside the noise
  at 3 seeds. They did not change the leak classes.
- **Staying in the mode adds +1.3.** The 3.12 quiet exit fired 25 to 28 times a battle: in
  most rounds the enemy stopped firing for 120 ticks and the duel took over. With `stay` the
  missed shields halve (3.8 to 1.6 a battle). Band 4 drops from +3.5 to +2.8, though, and 44
  battles of 267 end the mode at 150 damage, so a few robots do worse without the exits.
- **The body-turn base costs about 1.1.** It is a reconstruction, and it takes the predictor
  away on robots it does not fit. Leave SHIELD-12 off.
- **The largest leak left is `nowave`: about 12 hits a battle.** These are bullets that hit us
  with no wave of the mode's to match: the shot was never seen as a shot (an uncertain energy
  drop, or a scan gap with nothing before it). That is the next target, ahead of the predictors.
  Missed shields are now 1.6 a battle and unshielded ones 3.6.
- **Off-mode hits (7.6 a battle) come after the 150 battle exit.** Nullstride uses the same
  rule, but whether 150 suits Hadur is open: compare `stay` with its exit at SHIELD-6's 370
  next.
