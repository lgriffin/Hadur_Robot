# 3.12 P1 and P2: findings

Date: 2026-10-11. Stages P1 and P2 of `docs/plan-3.12.md` (`hadur-bench/plans/p312.queue`). Bench tooling, data and documents only; no change to `hadur-core` or `hadur-robot`, nothing released. The candidates are built by `build-ablation.sh` from the v3.11 tag (one-line edits), so they are not in the repository's source. Each is paired against the released Hadur 3.11 on the same opponents and seeds.

## What was run

- P1: `ne` (END-1 keeps the fighting distance), `f5` (DIST-1 floor 500 px), `nef5` (both), on `screen-311.txt` (400 census bots drawn in proportion to band size), seeds 501 and 502.
- P2: `nm` (MIR-1 off) on `volatile-312.txt` (the 38 all-or-nothing bots), 8 seeds (501-508).
- 35 rounds, Robocode 1.11.1, parallel 12, child CPUs 2, heap 2G, CPU constant 1488498, RoboRumble workers stopped. Step times: prep 92 s, `ne` 34 min, `f5` 35 min, `nef5` 35 min, `vol` 10 min; all five done 05:40.
- Rows: `data/bench/2026-10-11_hadur-p312-{ne,f5,nef5,vol}-local_cold.tsv`; per-round rows for `vol` only (`..._vol-local_cold_rounds.tsv`); reports `2026-10-11_p312-{ne,f5,nef5,vol}.md`. The other rounds files and per-opponent folders are not committed.
- Analysis: `data/tools/screen_paired.py` on each rows and rounds pair, `--set` as the step ran. Census row rules drop a battle with another Robocode JVM running (`otherJvms`: 215, 179 and 221 of 800 pairs in the three P1 steps, 39 of 304 in `vol`); no security denials and no duress in any row. The `--keep-all` reading skips the rule.

## Reading against the P1 gate

The gate asks for a paired mean of at least +0.15 APS with the interval above 0, no band below -0.05, and the top bands holding.

- `ne` is the nearest: +0.11 and +0.09 are below +0.15 and the intervals cross 0. Its gain sits in bands 2 and 3 (+0.28 combined under the census rules), the bands the plan says must hold, and it gives back -0.14 in band 5 under the census rules (-0.09 keep-all, band 5 below -0.05 either way). Fails.
- `f5` fails: negative total, band 6 significantly negative, band 5 at -0.055 under the census rules.
- `nef5` fails: about zero, the `ne` gain in bands 2 and 3 does not carry, band 5 at -0.12.
- No candidate goes to P3 on this evidence.
- `nm`: no sign that MIR-1 off helps the all-or-nothing pairings; the point estimate is a loss and the interval includes zero. These pairings are not shown to be a mirror-handling fault.

## Caveats

- Two seeds per opponent on 400 bots: the interval on a total is about ±0.35 APS, wider than the +0.15 gate. `ne`'s +0.09 to +0.11 can neither pass nor be ruled out as a real gain of that size.
- 22 to 28% of pairs are dropped by the `otherJvms` flag under the census rules, as in the M4 screen. It is the harness artifact described in the M1 findings; the two readings agree on which candidates fail.
- `nm` is built with the v3.11 ref and one edit; `vol` has 8 seeds on 38 bots and wide intervals (about ±4 points on a mean).
- Bench scores, not live; the plan's inference that distance costs bullets taken is not supported by `f5`, which moved the walk-in floor and found no gain.
