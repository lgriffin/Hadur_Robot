## Notes (S1)

- **Gate: within noise of S0.** Shadow score share is 46.2% ± 6.4 against 1.20's 40.8% ± 8.3; rounds won 98 against 82. The intervals overlap, and S1 is not meant to change play, so read this as noise, not progress. The shape is the same as S0: Hadur wins more than half the rounds but less than half the score, because Shadow out-damages it (38.6% bullet-damage share).
- **Same seed, same battle.** Battles are seeded. In the 4 battles that ran with no skipped turn under both 1.20 and 2.0, 3 have identical ground-truth logs for all 35 rounds, turn by turn. The fourth (RamFire seed 4) is one turn late on the first scan of the battle and matches 1.20's play otherwise (see below).
- **Two port slips were caught that way and fixed** before this run: 1.20's fire check read `getGunTurnRemaining()` in degrees, and 1.20's aim saw the gun heat from a shot fired earlier in the same tick (the engine's proxy updates `getGunHeat()` as soon as `setFireBullet` is called). The core now reproduces both.
- **Slower first scan.** On the first scan of a battle, 2.0 sometimes takes long enough that its orders land one turn late, with or without a reported skipped turn (class loading and first-use linking of the new records and lambdas). Skipped turns here total 73 against S0's 44, mostly against Shadow. The tick budget is S6 work; a warm-up at battle start is the cheap fix if it matters sooner.
- **Faults: 0** in 1,050 rounds. Hit rates are new in this report (RES-5).
