## Notes (S2)

- **Gate: phantom waves near zero, met.** Of 12,144 inferred waves across all opponents,
  8 had no real bullet behind them, and none against Shadow. The ledger explained away 1,820
  drops that 1.20 would have surfed as waves; against Crazy that was more than one false
  wave per real shot.
- **Gate: score share up or flat, met within noise.** The sample bots all went up, and Hadur won all
  875 of their rounds (S1: 866). Shadow is 43.7% ± 4.6 against S1's 46.2% ± 6.4; two
  earlier S2 runs scored 45.3% and 43.6%, so read Shadow as flat. The ledger rarely changes
  which waves Hadur surfs against Shadow (460 phantoms in 9,670 waves); the gains S2 unlocks
  come from S3 onward, which build on waves being real.
- **Wave fidelity is new.** The bench matches each `EW` record to the engine's real bullet
  (same round, within 3 turns and 0.15 power, maximum one-to-one matching). "Unseen" shots
  were fired while a robot was disabled and matched nothing. 55 visible Shadow shots (0.6%)
  were still missed; not yet examined.
- **Fixes the bench drove.** Drops of 0.1 read as 0.0999999 (now compared with a 1e-6 slack).
  Wall damage is taken at the speed the enemy could have struck with, because the engine
  applies acceleration before its wall check (an earlier run showed 13.6% false waves
  against Crazy). The bench's first unseen-shot rule counted seen shots as unseen.
- **Review fixes in this run.** Shots seen after missed scans now mark the last wave in the
  gap; braking to a stop over a scan gap is not taken as a wall hit; a shot fired while
  striking a wall still becomes a wave; the R record now carries radar reacquire ticks and
  hidden shots (RES-5): 82 reacquire ticks and 206 hidden shots found in 1,050 rounds.
- **RADAR-1.** In the Shadow diagnostic, 1.20's radar lost the enemy for the rest of a round
  after a skipped turn, and Hadur fought blind until it died. The core now sweeps back toward
  the last bearing. Skipped turns: 57 (S1: 73).
- **Faults: 0** in 1,050 rounds.
