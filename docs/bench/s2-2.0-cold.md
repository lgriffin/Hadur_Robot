## Notes (S2)

- **Gate: phantom waves near zero, met.** Of 11,955 inferred waves across all opponents,
  3 had no real bullet behind them (all against Crazy), and against Shadow none did. The
  ledger explained away 1,796 drops that 1.20 would have surfed as waves; against Crazy that
  was more than one false wave per real shot.
- **Gate: score share up or flat, met within noise.** The sample bots all went up, and Hadur won all
  875 of their rounds (S1: 866). Shadow is 43.6% ± 3.2 against S1's 46.2% ± 6.4. The
  intervals overlap, and an earlier S2 run with the same Shadow play scored 45.3% ± 4.8, so
  read Shadow as flat. The ledger rarely changes which waves Hadur surfs against Shadow (435
  phantoms in 9,485 waves); the gains S2 unlocks come from S3 onward, which build on waves
  being real.
- **Wave fidelity is new.** The bench matches each `EW` record to the engine's real bullet
  (same round, within 3 turns and 0.15 power). The fidelity table was recomputed from this
  run's logs after a fix to the bench's accounting: a disabled robot is still scanned, so a
  shot fired as the enemy dropped to 0 energy is often seen, and the first version counted
  those matches as false waves. Only unmatched shots from those moments are now "unseen".
- **Two ledger fixes came from the bench.** Drops of 0.1 read as 0.0999999 (now compared
  with a 1e-6 slack), and wall damage is taken at the speed the enemy could have hit the wall
  with, because the engine applies acceleration before its wall check. The first cold run,
  before that fix, showed 13.6% false waves against Crazy.
- **RADAR-1.** In the Shadow diagnostic, 1.20's radar lost the enemy for the rest of a round
  after a skipped turn, and Hadur fought blind until it died. The core now sweeps back toward
  the last bearing. Skipped turns fell to 53 from S1's 73, mostly against Shadow (20 from 36).
- **Faults: 0** in 1,050 rounds.
