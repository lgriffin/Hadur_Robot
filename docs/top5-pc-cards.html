CARD 2 · STAGE F

Free points: the start-up disable

about 1 h 30 min

In the census, Hadur was disabled at the start of round 0 in 0.8% of battles: it never took a turn in that round. The engine gives each robot 300 × the CPU constant to start a round (447 ms on the bench). If Hadur's set-up runs longer, the engine skips turns until it disables Hadur. This card finds out whether that comes from the opponents or from the long run, and tests one fix.

Runs

  • stall: builds the panel from the census logs. It lists every opponent with a start-up disable, about 85 bots.
  • F: 4 seeds, arms c, aa and xw, 12 workers unpinned (the census setting), at the start of a fresh run.
  • F-read: writes docs/bench/local/<date>_top5-F.md.

Measures

  • Start-up disables per arm on these opponents.
  • Set-up time per arm from the new SETUP record, split into warm-up, core and memory.
  • The xw arm against c and aa: score and disables.

What the result decides

ship

xw cuts start-up disables here, scores no lower than c (interval above −0.5 points), and passes its Stage 0 guard. It goes into the next release without a census.

rebuild

c still stalls, but SETUP shows the long part is the core or the memory load, not the warm-up. xw is the wrong fix, so a fix for that part comes first.

close

c stalls in under 1% of battles at the start of a fresh run. The cause is the long run, not these opponents. No robot fix is needed, and the bench gets fresh worker homes between blocks instead.

Expected size: 0.02 to 0.03 APS on the bench. Live it is not measured yet. The next release carries the SETUP record and the start-up fields in the health record, and the live watch writes them to startup.tsv.

CARD 3 · STAGE 0

Calibration: how many workers the PC can run

about 5 h 30 min

The panel is 208 opponents: every bot in bands 1 and 2, and 40 from each other band. Each block runs the same seeds with four arms per job, in shuffled order: c, aa, hv (heaviest per turn) and xw (Stage F's fix, guarded here on ordinary opponents). The blocks run in this order:

StepWorkersPinnedBattlesAboutWhy
s0-u1212no8321 h 20today's setting
s0-p1212yes8321 h 20what pinning alone does
s0-p1818yes83255 minmore throughput
s0-p2222yes83245 minthe most the 48 processors pin
s0-p12b12yes8321 h 20drift check against p12

Measures, per block

  • Battles an hour, skipped turns a round, share of trusted battles
  • Start-up disables and set-up time per arm
  • c's panel level, A/A difference against its band, hv and xw against the pooled reference

Output

s0-read writes docs/bench/local/<date>_top5-s0.md, with one section per block and a side-by-side table.

What the result decides

pick

Use the largest worker count whose skipped turns a round stay within 15% of p12, and whose c level and hv difference agree with p12. If 22 passes, every later stage runs about 1.7 times faster.

stop

The A/A pair falls outside its 95% interval in more than one block, or p12 and p12b disagree beyond their noise. That is a bench defect, so the plan stops until it is found.
