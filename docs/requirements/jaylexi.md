# Requirements: JayLexi (MiniRumble)

JayLexi, the MiniRumble 1v1 entry.

EARS requirements, one row each; [README](README.md) gives the rules. IDs never change meaning and are never reused; a requirement that is dropped moves to [retired.md](retired.md).

| ID | Pattern | Requirement | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| JAYLEXI-1 | Unwanted | If Robocode's codesize tool reports more than 1,496 bytes for the shipped jar (MiniRumble's 1,499 less 3 spare), then the build shall fail. | K6 |
| JAYLEXI-2 | Event | When the enemy is scanned, the bot shall turn its radar twice the turn to the enemy, so it scans it every tick. | K1 |
| JAYLEXI-3 | Event | When the enemy's energy drops by more than 0.09 and at most 3, after our hits on it (less their damage) and its hits on us (plus three times their power) are booked, the bot shall launch an enemy wave from where the enemy was a tick earlier at that bullet's speed. | K3 |
| JAYLEXI-4 | Event | When the enemy is scanned, the bot shall launch a gun wave segmented on velocity history, lateral speed, walls ahead and distance, aim at its most visited of 47 bins, and log the enemy's bin, weighted 5 for a fired wave, when the wave passes it. | K2, J6 |
| JAYLEXI-5 | State | While an enemy wave is in flight, the bot shall predict back and forward to the wave's arrival and orbit the less dangerous way, danger being the logged bin over the distance to the enemy to the fourth, plus a quarter of the same for the second nearest wave when there is one (0.2); while none is, it shall drive straight away from the enemy. (The plan's stop option measured 2 APS worse, docs/kitlab.md.) | J1 |
| JAYLEXI-6 | Event | When hit by a bullet, or when bullets collide, the bot shall log the guess factor of the hit, smoothed over the neighbouring bins, on the enemy wave that was passing it, and stop surfing that wave. | J2 |
| JAYLEXI-7 | Ubiquitous | The bot shall turn its drive until a 140 px stick from it stays inside the field. | J3 |
| JAYLEXI-8 | Event | When the gun is within 3 degrees of its aim, the bot shall fire 3 inside 150 px and 2 beyond, at most a fifteenth of its own energy and at most what finishes the enemy. | J4, K4 |
| JAYLEXI-9 | Ubiquitous | The shipped jar shall carry the robot's properties at the release version and its source. | plan S5 |
| JAYLEXI-10 | Ubiquitous | On the MiniRumble bench sample, paired against Komarious 1.88, the build shall predict at least 87.7 APS before it is submitted. | plan S4 |
| JAYLEXI-11 | Ubiquitous | The shipped code shall give the same orders, bit for bit, as the readable reference kept with the tests, on seeded replays of scans, hits and shots, so bytes are saved without changing play. | 0.2 |
| JAYLEXI-19 | Ubiquitous | The bot shall turn its gun by a random angle of up to 0.125 rad either way against a robot whose orbit char carries 2 pi more than its orbit (rsim.mini.BulletCatcher 0.4, whose catch it breaks: +57.3 ± 3.3 points, docs/bench/local/2026-11-04_jaylexi-jit.md), and aim as before against every other robot; the shipped jar is built through ProGuard (`jaylexi/proguard.pro`, 1,494 B) and JayLexiJarIT replays it against the readable reference. | 0.6 |
| JAYLEXI-20 | Event | When an enemy wave is launched, the bot shall also segment the wave's surf stats on how many of three points along our orbit ahead (0.18, 0.36 and 0.64 rad round the enemy, at the current distance) are still inside the field, counted until the first one outside (0 to 3); the gun's own wall count uses the same method from our side. (Walls segment, about +0.40 over 0.6 on four fresh 3 to 6 seed cloud sets, fitted at 1,495 B: docs/bench/jaylexi-own-movement.md.) | 0.7 |
| JAYLEXI-21 | Ubiquitous | The bot shall read its orbit angle by opponent and by distance (one slot per 50 px in ORB, at the opponent's AIM row) at no extra code bytes, with its own angle for RammingC (0.75), LightningBug (0.95) and MirrorMicro (1.35), and an orbit 0.4 rad further out inside 200 px for Bruce and Lifestealer, each locked on 12 fresh cloud seeds (docs/bench/jaylexi-tailoring.md). | 0.8 |
| JAYLEXI-22 | Ubiquitous | The bot shall count its danger prediction (where its orbit takes it before a wave arrives) from tick 2, where 0.8 counted from 3, so each predicted spot is taken one tick further along the path. (+0.29 ± 0.23 over 0.8 on 12 fresh cloud seeds over 112 MiniRumble robots, 0 code bytes: docs/bench/jaylexi-tailoring.md.) | 0.9 |
| JAYLEXI-23 | Event | When an enemy wave is launched, the bot shall keep its surf stats in one distance band, where 0.9 split them into four (under 150, 350 and 550 px), so every wave learns from every other whatever the range. (+0.23 ± 0.20 over 0.9 on a 12-seed cloud lock over 112 MiniRumble robots, +0.56 ± 0.24 head to head on 12 more, 9 code bytes fewer: docs/bench/jaylexi-tailoring.md.) | 1.0 |
| JAYLEXI-24 | Event | When its gun has turned to within 1200 / distance degrees of the aim, the bot shall fire, where 1.0 waited for 3 degrees at every range: wider inside 400 px, so it fires sooner at robots that close in, narrower beyond. (+0.35 ± 0.26 over 1.0 on a 12-seed cloud lock over 112 MiniRumble robots, about +0.3 over 24 seeds; +3 to +4 points on chargers: docs/bench/jaylexi-tailoring.md.) | 1.1 |
| JAYLEXI-25 | State | While the enemy is FoilistMC 1.0 or CunobelinDC 1.2 and at least 500 px away, the bot shall fire power 1, where it fires 2 at every other robot: these strong learners out-last it on energy at range. Power is read from a row per robot, like the aim and orbit rows. (+6.62 ± 2.21 on FoilistMC over 24 fresh cloud seeds, +2.64 ± 2.02 on CunobelinDC over 48; as a field rule it lost -0.51: docs/bench/jaylexi-tailoring.md section 12.) | 1.2 |
