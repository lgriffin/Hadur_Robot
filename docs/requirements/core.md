# Requirements: Core, architecture and resilience

The hexagonal core, its values and ports, determinism, memory, the conductor's roles and strands, the World, the team link and the build's own guarantees. These hold in every style of battle.

EARS requirements, one row each; [README](README.md) gives the rules. IDs never change meaning and are never reused; a requirement that is dropped moves to [retired.md](retired.md).

| TIME-11 | Ubiquitous | The robot's test code shall carry `hadur117.TickPerf`, a tick-time replay: it plays recorded battles (the bench's `--record` transcripts) through a core built as the robot builds it (the shipped shield list and mode table, the store the battle started on, the round-end, checkpoint and battle-end calls where recorded), reading each transcript a line at a time so its heap holds only what the core holds, and for every recording and pass prints the ticks, mean, p50, p90, p99, p99.9 and largest tick time, the ticks over 70% and 100% of the allowance (the live CPU constant 3,272,413 ns by default), the bytes the core allocated a tick, the GC collections and pause time of the pass, the slowest round-end call, a digest of every replayed order and the ticks whose orders differ from the recorded ones; `--ticks-out DIR` writes every tick's time and bytes. | TIME-12 | Ubiquitous | The core shall allocate less per tick without changing a single decision: the robot state log shall key states by tick in a map that keeps no boxed key and no entry object yet iterates exactly as `HashMap<Long, RobotState>` did (`TickMap`), looking a tick up once instead of twice; the KD-tree shall reuse its search heap; a wave's precise intersection shall gather its angles in a plain array; bullet shadows shall reuse three points; the field gun shall play a trajectory forward with one offset buffer and build only the final point, and sum a solution's per-target weights only when it is a new best; team risk shall clip a bullet segment against a box over plain doubles and reuse two bullet points. | TIME-13 | Ubiquitous | The robot's tests shall fail when a duel (sample.Walls), melee (melee-samples) or team (MyFirstTeam member 1) fixture, replayed warm through TIME-11's replay, allocates more than 35, 70 or 210 KB a tick on the least of three warm passes (1.3 to 2 times what each allocated when TIME-12 shipped, far under the 60, 364 and 795 KB before it) or plays any tick differently from its recording. Rationale: the per-tick garbage is what makes the GC pauses behind skipped turns, so it must not grow unnoticed. | R9 |
| TIME-14 | Ubiquitous | The core's close-range hot paths shall do the same arithmetic in the same order with less work and garbage: the rammer escape (RAM-5, RAM-20) shall play its candidate paths on plain coordinates, read the shots in flight from an array filled once a tick, test a shot's aims or rays only when the shot's flown stretch can reach the ring about the path's point, and stop a path once its closest approach less the penalty already incurred is under the best's; the predictor shall step a multi-tick prediction and the precise escape angle on plain coordinates; a gun mimic shall build each segment's profile once between learns; a projected displacement vector shall iterate on plain coordinates. Every recording shall replay with zero differing ticks (TIME-11). Rationale: at tight timing the close-range ticks run over the turn; RamTrackSurfer's mean tick fell from 435 to 179 us, its p99 from 1.5 to 0.5 ms and its garbage from 83 to 20 KB a tick. | R10 |
| TIME-15 | Event | When the engine skips one of Hadur's turns, the core shall count the skip and shed no computation for it: no level for the rest of the round and no allowance learned from the tick before it; only TIME-1's slow tick sheds, against the adapter's fixed 3 ms allowance. Rationale: the engine's allowance is the client's own CPU constant, so our computation takes the same share of it on any client, and a skip is a stall outside our tick (garbage collection, a loaded host); shedding for the round turned each stall into hundreds of weaker turns. At 0.5x the live constant on 30 robots, 4 seeds, shedding nothing scored +0.39 +- 0.45 share against master (A/A -0.10 +- 0.52). | R10 |
