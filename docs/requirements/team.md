# Requirements: Team

The team entry: the team mode table and the tactics that run only with a living teammate.

EARS requirements, one row each; [README](README.md) gives the rules. IDs never change meaning and are never reused; a requirement that is dropped moves to [retired.md](retired.md).

| ID | Pattern | Requirement | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| TMODE-1 | State | While in melee on a team with a living teammate, movement shall play the team modes the mode table holds for the first living enemy whose class (its name without version or duplicate marker) the table names, or, for a team the table does not name, the modes the build forces on (none in the shipped build). | T7 |
| TMODE-2 | Optional | Where the DODGE mode is played without OWN (TMODE-27), a detected shot shall count for Hadur by 1/(1+k)² for the k living teammates nearer its source than Hadur was when it was fired. | T7 |
| TMODE-3 | Optional | Where the GANG mode is played, while two or more enemies seen within the cap age are within 250 px of Hadur, each candidate's risk shall be scaled by e^(0.5 (to − from) / 100) for its and Hadur's distances to the nearest living teammate; otherwise, while that holds for a living teammate, by the same for the nearest such teammate, each distance floored at 300 px. The posture's term shall not be scaled. | T7 |
| TMODE-4 | Optional | Where the FINISH mode is played, while the engine counts one enemy alive, that enemy was seen within the cap age, and Hadur has 30 energy or more, each candidate's risk shall be scaled by e^((to − from) / 100) for its and Hadur's distances to that enemy, each floored at 350 px, in place of TMODE-3. | T7 |
| TMODE-7 | Optional | Where the DODGE mode is played (TMODE-27), MMOVE-10's learning and learned aims shall keep one log per enemy class (the name without version or duplicate marker) fed by the hits of every copy of that class. | T7 |
| TMODE-9 | Optional | Where the MATEFIRE mode is played, each bullet a teammate reports firing shall count in the member's destination risk as an enemy's virtual bullet does, on the reported heading, until it has passed the member. | T7 |
| TMODE-11 | Optional | Where the DODGE mode is played (TMODE-27), the member shall re-rank its eight lowest-risk destinations, and the destination it holds, by the damage expected from the shots in flight its 30-tick drive to each would cross, each shot a profile over its virtual bullets' headings and its escape angle summing to its aimed-at weight, and shall take the least of risk over the median risk plus three times damage over the median damage (floored at 0.1). | T7 |
| TMODE-12 | Ubiquitous | The team mode table's default, for a team it does not name, shall include the MATEFIRE mode. | T7 |
| TMODE-13 | Optional | Where the HUNT mode is played, while Hadur has 20 energy or more, the members (in name order) shall each be assigned the fresh enemy of least (distance + 1.2 × energy)², times 1.6 for an enemy three members already hunt, and Hadur's destination risk, all terms but the bullets' and the posture's, shall be scaled by e^(0.3 (to − from) / 100) for its and Hadur's distances to its place 350 px from its prey, ranked by bearing among the members on that prey and spread up to 70° apart. | T7 |
| TMODE-14 | Ubiquitous | The team mode table's default, for a team it does not name, shall include the HUNT mode. | T7 |
| TMODE-19 | Ubiquitous | The team link shall be able to carry a member's gun heading and gun heat in its report (link version 3), and shall read such a report back exactly; Hadur's own reports shall leave it out, so they are written as before. The GUNLINE mode that read it failed (pc-team-gunline) and is removed. | T7 |
| TMODE-20 | Optional | Where the team mode table names OWN against a team, while Hadur has a living teammate, a shot by that team shall make virtual bullets only when no teammate stood 0.9 times nearer its source than Hadur (MMOVE-9's nearest rule). | T7 |
| TMODE-21 | Optional | Where the team mode table names HEADON against a team, while Hadur has a living teammate, each shot by that team shall make one head-on virtual bullet of weight 2, and no linear one. | T7 |
| TMODE-22 | Optional | Where the team mode table names LINEAR against a team, while Hadur has a living teammate, each shot by that team shall make one linear virtual bullet of weight 2, and no head-on one. | T7 |
| TMODE-25 | Optional | Where the PATH mode is played against a team, while Hadur has a living teammate with a bullet in flight, the destination shall be chosen among the 8 lowest-risk candidates by their risk plus the damage of each teammate bullet that, on Hadur's straight drive there (accelerating 1 px a tick to 8) over the next 15 ticks, crosses Hadur's box 18 px each side; a held destination not yet reached shall be kept unless another scores below 0.9 times its risk plus its own path damage. | T7 |
| TMODE-27 | Optional | Where the DODGE mode is played against a team, while Hadur has a living teammate, each detected enemy shot shall be turned once, when first seen, into a profile: the simple aims (head-on, linear, circular, linear at the 16-tick average velocity, linear at half speed) at Hadur's state when it was fired and the team's learned aims pooled by enemy class, each carrying its expert's weight exp(-loss) against that class, where an expert's loss decays by 0.95 a hit and grows by 0 within one bot width of each enemy bullet that hit a member and up to 1 at three, the hit member's errors travelling with its link sample; the shot shall count for Hadur by 1/(1+k)^2 for the k teammates nearer its source (by the nearest rule where the table names OWN); the destination shall be the best of the 12 lowest-risk candidates and the held one by risk/median + 3 x damage/median, the damage being the profiles crossed driving there for 30 ticks plus the teammates' bullets crossed in 15, the held one times 0.9; the base risk then counts no bullet. | T7 |
| TMODE-28 | Ubiquitous | The team mode table's default, for a team it does not name, shall include the PATH mode, and every team the table names shall play PATH on top of its entry. | T7 |
| TMODE-29 | Optional | Where DODGE's own-fire switch is on, DODGE's base ranking shall keep MATEFIRE's teammate-bullet risk, and each re-ranked destination's risk shall gain PATH's factor times the damage of the teammates' bullets crossed on its 15-tick drive, those bullets no longer counting in its wave damage. | T7 |
| TMODE-30 | Ubiquitous | The team mode table's default, for a team it does not name, shall include the DODGE mode, every team the table names shall play DODGE on top of its entry, and DODGE's own-fire switch shall be on. | T7 |
| TMODE-33 | Optional | Where the team modes against the opposing team carry CONCORD (the team brain after Ogi's design, written our own way), HadurTeam shall: score each enemy's risk at a point by 1/(1 + the robots, other enemies seen lately and teammates, nearer that enemy than the point) in place of the closest-robot factor; play FINISH; play GANG once 200 or more member-turns this battle (Hadur and each teammate in view, each tick) show 4% or more with two enemies within 250 px; draw each enemy's gun situations from every history of its class; and fire at most 1.9 beyond 200 px unless it has hit that class on 55% of 20 or more shots. CONCORD shall ship table-only and off, and the `tmconcord` ablation (suffix cn) shall force it on with duel and melee play unchanged; it keeps the hunt and the shipped radar (the group radar and the hunt stand-down lost in the first cloud smoke and are removed), and the `tmconcordm`, `tmconcordg` and `tmconcordp` ablations weigh one part each. | T7 |
| TMODE-34 | Ubiquitous | The team mode table's default, for a team it does not name, shall include the CONCORD mode, and every team the table names shall play CONCORD on top of its entry. | T7 |
| TMODE-35 | Ubiquitous | The team mode table shall name OgiTeam, FirestarterTeam and NightmareTeam's member classes with every mode they played before without CONCORD; CombatTeam, named without it until pc-team-pack3 (PR #1033), shall play CONCORD. | T7 |
| TMODE-42 | Ubiquitous | Where the team modes against a team carry CLOSE with HUNT, a member's hunt place (TMODE-13) shall be `TeamRisk.HUNT_CLOSE` (250) px from its prey instead of 350; CLOSE without HUNT shall hunt nothing. CLOSE shall be in every team's modes: the default through `FORCE_CLOSE` and every shipped table entry, as pc-team-close measured it (BENCH-205, PR #696: +0.56 ± 0.42 APS over 45 teams at 12 seeds, own-member bullet hits +4.2 ± 1.7 a battle accepted); ablation tmnoclose (suffix ncl) is the build before. | T7 |
| TMODE-45 | Complex | Where the team modes against a team carry QUARTERS, while Hadur has a living teammate and an armed enemy (energy above zero, scanned within `CAP_MAX_AGE` ticks) is inside `MinimumRiskMovement.QUARTERS_RANGE` (200) px and driving toward the member, the nearest such enemy (the chaser) shall not cap the candidate ring (MMOVE-1, MMOVE-7), a destination inside `QUARTERS_KEEP` (150) px of it shall pay `QUARTERS_K` (10) at the chaser falling in a line to nothing at 150 px, added after any pull, and the drive shall keep full speed through a turn over 45 degrees. With no chaser, without QUARTERS, or off a team the decision shall be today's. QUARTERS shall not be in the default modes (`FORCE_QUARTERS` off); of the shipped entries only NightmareTeam's member classes (`ustimaw.Nightmare`, `ustimaw.NigitmareDroid`) shall carry it, as pc-team-quarters measured it (BENCH-225, PR #759: +4.0 ± 3.2 share points on 24 seeds and +4.3 ± 3.7 on 16 fresh ones, aimed damage from under 150 px down 25%); ablation tmquarters (suffix qt) plays it on every team. | T7 |
| TMODE-47 | Complex | Where the team modes against the enemies carry LAST, while one enemy is alive by the filtered count (WORLD-8) and a teammate lives, the core shall keep the Melee role (ROLE-3) instead of handing each member to the Duel, and the team movement shall draw the member to that enemy at `TeamRisk.FINISH_FLOOR` (350) px with `FINISH_PULL`, while it was scanned within `CAP_MAX_AGE` ticks and the member's energy is at least `FINISH_ENERGY` (30). Once no teammate lives the member shall hand over to the Duel as today (MELEE-2, MMEM-2). LAST shall be in the default modes (`FORCE_LAST` on) and on every shipped entry, as pc-team-guard measured it (BENCH-237, PR #790: +0.41 ± 0.44 APS over 45 teams at 12 seeds, own-member bullet hits 86 to 50 a battle), shipped on the owner's call with the gate's low end at -0.03; ablation tmnolast (suffix nla) turns it off. | T7 |
| TMODE-49 | Complex | Where the team modes against a team carry MOMENTUM, while Hadur has a living teammate, each candidate the wave re-rank (TMODE-11, TMODE-27) or the teammates'-bullet path check (TMODE-25) drives to shall be simulated from the member's present heading and velocity under the engine's movement rules (turn rate 10 - 0.75 x speed degrees a tick, acceleration 1, deceleration 2, backwards when that is the shorter turn, speed capped at 4 through a turn over 45 degrees unless QUARTERS keeps full speed, a wall stopping the member along its heading; `Momentum.drive`) instead of from a standstill pointed at the candidate. Without MOMENTUM, or off a team, the decision shall be today's. MOMENTUM shall be in the default modes (`FORCE_MOMENTUM` on) and on every shipped entry, as pc-team-momentum measured it (BENCH-247, PR #826: the drive alone +0.44 ± 0.43 APS over 45 teams at 12 fresh seeds; with full speed through turns as well +0.34 ± 0.46, failed, that part removed); ablation tmnomomentum (suffix nmo) turns it off. Our own design after the 2026-11-15 read of OgiTeam. | T7 |
| TMODE-50 | State | While Hadur has a living teammate and the DODGE mode is played, each detected shot by an enemy whose class the fitted-aim table (`TeamModes.FITTED`) names shall add to its profile one aim at that class's fitted heading, bearing(source, P) + f(d) asin(v_lat / s) for Hadur's position P and lateral velocity v_lat the class's lag of ticks before the fire tick, s its fitted speed (or the bullet's), f(d) = a + b d / 1200, at the class's share of the profile, the other aims scaled to the rest. FITTED shall be on in the shipped build (`FORCE_FITTED`), as pc-team-fitted measured it (BENCH-254, PR #865: +0.32 ± 0.10 APS spread over 46 pairings at 24 fresh seeds on the eight fitted teams, every team at or above its A/A); ablation tmnofitted (suffix nfi) turns it off. Each entry comes from reading the class's gun code and fitting its aim on bench bullet logs (`tools/team_aimfit.py`). | T7 |
| TMODE-52 | State | While Hadur has a living teammate and the DODGE mode is played, and an enemy wave's front will land on Hadur within `TeamDodge.CLOSE_TICKS` (35) ticks, the dodge shall drive its `TeamDodge.CLOSE_RERANK` (40) lowest-risk candidates through the waves instead of `RERANK` (12). On in the shipped build (`TeamModes.FORCE_CLOSERERANK`), as pc-team-closerr measured it (BENCH-257, PR #919: +0.57 ± 0.41 APS spread over 46 pairings at 12 fresh seeds on all 46 teams, scored against both baselines; skipped turns 22.4 a battle against 6.4); ablation tmnocloserr (suffix ncr) turns it off. Duel and melee untouched. | T7 |
